# `we` inclusive/exclusive audit (2026-08-30)

Scope: independent/free first-person plural pronouns only. Forms are ordered inclusive, then
exclusive. A Grambank `GB028=1` value establishes that a contrast exists, but is not by itself a
form-level citation; a row stays `UNKNOWN` here until both forms and a defensible broad IPA can be
checked. This is why the unresolved list is longer than the list Grambank leaves unclassified.

## Review pass — three cells brought back into their row's convention

The doc above states the right rule ("the IPA is broad and follows the existing row conventions").
Three of the thirteen slipped it, in each case by writing the *better* form in isolation and making
the cell the only one of its kind in its row. Corrected, keeping the finding in every case:

| row | as written | corrected to | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pll` | `အယ် / ယဲ` | `ʔɛ / jɛ` | The row's declared script is Burmese but all ~40 of its other cells are bare IPA. One Burmese cell among forty is the defect logged for `rif`, `shi` and `syl` in handoff 46. The script question is real and is a whole-row job. |
| `cag` | `cas-vaatsha / yi-vaatshe-elh` /kaswaːtʃa / jiwaːtʃeel/ | `kaswaatʃa / jiwaatʃeeɬ` | Same shape: every other Nivaclé cell is IPA-as-surface (`jiwaatʃa`, `weʔɬa`, `xpɔjitʃ`), including the `i` cell this exclusive is built from. The IPA also romanised ⟨lh⟩ as `l`; Nivaclé ⟨lh⟩ is /ɬ/, which that row's own `weʔɬa` already shows. |
| `chy` | /nɪnɪːhovɪma / nanɪːhovɪmɪ/ | /neneːhovema / naneːhoveme/ | Phonetically the better call — Cheyenne ⟨e⟩ is [ɪ] — but all 45 other cells in the row write /e/, so this made `we` the only cell with ɪ. Worth doing to the whole row with a source; not to one cell. |

Everything else was taken as written. Two are independently confirmed: **Rotokas** `vigei / igei`
matches a contrast extracted from the Rotokas NT (1 John 1:9 against 1 John 1:3 and Acts 4:20), and
**Macushi** `anna` as the exclusive matches the same test on the Macushi NT.

**`kry` is the most valuable of the thirteen** and deserves calling out: the old cell `vin` was not a
first-person pronoun at all. Lezgian *čun* 'we' → Kryz *žin* and Lezgian *kün* 'you (pl)' → Kryz
*vin*, so the atlas had that row's **2PL** filed under "we", beside a correct 1SG `zin` and 2SG `vun`.
That is the class of error no typological database can catch, because it is about which word, not
which feature.

**One pre-existing row defect surfaced by the `pcc` cell.** Bouyei's tone letters do not map
consistently in this atlas: ⟨l⟩ is ˨˩ in `gul` but ˧˥ in `ndeeul`, and ⟨z⟩ is ˩ in `mengz` and `rauz`
but ˨˦ in `ndeengz`. The new `dul /du˧˥/` follows `ndeeul`. Whichever is right, the row needs one
mapping and currently has two; `tools/tone_digit_map_check.js` does not cover Bouyei.
