# Han Map review 88 — juc row against Grube 1896 (2026-11-06)

Review 87 left the row as mostly a Manchu copy with no open source. Grube, Die Sprache und Schrift der Jučen (1896), is public domain and on archive.org (11793168bsb). Its Jučen-deutsches Glossar (pp. 89–103) was read from the page scans, because the OCR is unreliable.

Rule used: Grube's syllables, read with the row's letters. A cell changes only when the form differs in structure. Differences a Chinese transcription cannot show (l/r, o/u, t/d, k/g) do not count. Grube's bracketed second reading [kā] lets gala, angga and duka stand.
