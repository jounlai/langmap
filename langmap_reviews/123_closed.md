# LangMap データレビュー #123 — Ainu (ain) row rebuilt from the Saru dictionaries (rally follow-up, 2026-11-14)

Draft: ~/langmap-work/rally1114/rebuild/ain (lm_fix.json, 98 of 100 sentences; #100 kept; #80 held).
Sources: Tamura Suzuko, アイヌ語沙流方言辞典 (1996) and 萱野茂のアイヌ語辞典 (1996), via the National Ainu Museum archive's public
word search (robots allow; ~120 sequential lookups); Wiktionary for numerals. Every non-obvious word is tagged [T]/[K] in the
evidence.
- エアイカプ 'cannot' was used for "must" (#37 #39 #54 #64) → クニ ㇷ゚ ネ.
- Person and possessive prefixes restored (クアニ ハポ 'I mother' → クコㇿ ハポ 'my mother'; ku= / e= on verbs).
- All wrong-gloss words from review 112 and many more replaced (ヌマン, オンネ, キサラ 'ear' → キサ 'train', シヌタプカ
  place name → シヌマ 'he/she', ミナ 'laugh' → シノッ 'play', ヌイェ 'carve' → エヤイパカㇱヌ 'study' / ヌカㇻ 'read' …); English loans
  (ステーション, パーク, テレホン …) → Ainu words or loans Tamura records in Ainu form.
- One spelling: Tamura's kana with small final consonants (ㇰ ㇱ ㇷ゚ ㇺ ㇻ), as the Word Map ain row; join marks split prefixes
  without changing the text.
- #93 マラット アン 'a festival is held' (Tamura's idiom) replaces review 83's ア=キ.
Held: #80 (no Saru word for 'experience' / 'valuable'). Loans where Ainu has no word (exam, week, Monday, clock hours ジ) — the
loan policy for every row is an open question. No native or second reader has seen it.
