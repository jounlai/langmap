- ᄆᆞᄅᆞᆯ 'the horse (acc.)' was used for "language" (#6 #10 #22 #57) → 마ᄅᆞᆯ (MK 말 'language' vs ᄆᆞᆯ 'horse').
- "-고 잇ᄂᆞ니라" used as a progressive in 9 cells (in MK it marked a lasting state) → present -ᄂᆞ-.
- Wrong-meaning words: 잇븐 'weary' for interesting, 잇다감 'now and then' for continuously, 호ᄅᆞ 'one day' for 'by himself',
  벗곳 'friend-flower' for cherry (→ 멎), ᄐᆞ다 'ride' for playing an instrument (→ ᄩᆞ다), ᄇᆡ ᄃᆞᆫ 곧 'harbour' for airport,
  귀ᄒᆞ다 'precious' for expensive (→ 빋ᄉᆞ다), 다ᅀᆞᆷ for next (→ 버거).
- 15th-century spelling row-wide (연철, vowel-harmonic particles, ᇰ ᄫ ᅀ ᅌ), first-person -오-/-우-, "must" -어ᅀᅡ ᄒᆞ다, no modern
  quoting 고; modern forms replaced (그들 → 그 사ᄅᆞᆷᄃᆞᆶ, 들 → ᄃᆞᆶ, 녁밥 → 나조ᄒᆡ 바ᄇᆞᆯ, 감긔 → 곳블 …); never-real forms replaced
  (먹ᄒᆞ리라, 축제ᄀᆞ, 나모에셔 → 남ᄀᆡ, 선ᄆᆞᆺ → 도산 …); clock times as double-hours and 刻; modern things by transparent
  paraphrase (거믄 湯 'black decoction' for coffee, ᄂᆞᄂᆞᆫ ᄇᆡ for aeroplane), as ja_heian and zh_tang do.
