# LangMap データレビュー #127 — White Hmong (hmn) and Manchu (mnc) rows rebuilt (rally follow-up, 2026-11-15)

hmn (89 sentences; 4 kept; 7 held): White Hmong RPA kept. ~45 sentences had Japanese order (verb last, modal last, time phrase between
subject and verb) → SVO per N. White, A Grammar of Hmong (2021/2025); classifier–noun–adjective–demonstrative order; wrong words
(tshais 'breakfast' for dinner, zoo 'good' for sunny, "uas tig" 'that turns' for across from, "huv si" 'all' for clean, khiav 'run
away' for move house, kev sau ntawv 'writing' for programming …); Nyiv Pooj; Hnub Ib for Monday. Sources: White 2021, wol.jw.org
White Hmong library, Tatoeba mww, Wiktionary. Held: #64 #77 #98 #63 #66 #38 #53 #92 #15 #35 #12 #91.
mnc (78 sentences; 11 kept; 11 held): all wrong meanings from review 115 replaced (tungken 'drum', gasambi 'lament', feniyen 'flock',
goro 'far', ilgambi 'distinguish', ahūra, alin, inu) and more (antaha 'guest' for hotel, poo 'cannon' for shop, habšan nomun
'lawsuit scripture' for phone, turi 'bean' for swim, baitakū 'useless' for works); grammar (-ci acambi, polite -reo/-rao,
progressive -me bi, amuran de, seme, -rengge, baharakū; non-forms jere, gisurehabi); pronoun i for he/she; two Mongolian I letters
(U+1822 → U+1873); Japan žiben. Sources: Wiktionary Manchu, Mini Buleku Sibe dictionary, Möllendorff 1892. Modern things as
transparent periphrases (tuwa sejen 'fire cart' for train), marked probable. No native reader for either row.
