# Name Map review 06 — frequency bands sourced (2026-11-12)

Open item: many forms carried an unchecked default 'moderate'. An agent checked 1,240 of 1,374 forms against official name statistics
(ONS England & Wales 2025, CSO Ireland, SSB Norway, SCB Sweden, US SSA via CRAN babynames, Israel CBS via CRAN, pl/cs Wikipedia register
counts, Bielefeld's German top 500), Wikipedia's most-popular-names tables, and forebears.io (living population, 2014; robots.txt
allows these pages). Behind the Name and INSEE downloads (robots-barred) were not used. Proposals with evidence per form:
~/langmap-work/nmfreq/proposals.json.
Bands: very-common = top 20 for its sex in recent births or ~top 40 of the living population; common = top 100 (~top 200 overall);
rare = under ~1 per 25,000 or religious use only; moderate = in use outside those.
Applied: the 436 high- and medium-confidence changes (232 high, 204 medium). Not applied: 182 low-confidence downgrades (forebears only;
it under-counts names popular since ~1995, e.g. Italy Matteo, Mexico Sofía). 134 forms could not be checked.
Distribution now: very-common 483, common 417, moderate 351, rare 123 (was 289 / 477 / 525 / 83).
Notes: religious-only cells (Vietnamese saints' names, Hui scripture names, Korean baptismal names not used as given names, Thai Phra Ram,
Nang Sida, Phra Phikhanet) are rare by the definition — the cell shows the community's form, the band its national usage; Sweden keeps
the Bible form Noak (rare; Noah is #1), Germany Sophie.
