One dark half of one day, and the Sinosphere has four different compounds for it.

Cantonese 夜晚. Hokkien 暗暝. Hakka 暗晡夜. Mandarin 晚上.

All four are written in Chinese characters. The characters themselves — 夜, 晚, 暗, 暝, 晡 — are shared property across the whole region. And still the combinations came out four different ways.

The script was shared. The words were not. That shows up most clearly in the most ordinary vocabulary.

Indo-European went the other way on this one. Proto-Indo-European *nókʷts survives almost intact: Latin nox, Greek νύξ, Slavic ночь, English night, German Nacht.

One family held a single word for six thousand years. The other built four from the same characters. The night is the same length everywhere.
