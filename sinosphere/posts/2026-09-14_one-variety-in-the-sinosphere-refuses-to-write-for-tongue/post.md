One variety in the Sinosphere refuses to write 舌 for "tongue".

Cantonese uses 脷 instead — a character invented for the purpose.

The reason is sound. In Cantonese, 舌 sounds like the word for "loss". Every mention of your tongue at the dinner table would carry that echo. So a different character was made to avoid it.

Rewriting the script because a word sounds unlucky is not unheard of in the Sinosphere. Doing it for a body part is striking.

And this is a word half the world also uses for "language": Latin lingua, Arabic لسان, Turkish dil, Slavic язык, Hungarian nyelv.

The organ in your mouth becomes the name for what you say with it. A word that close to home is exactly the kind you would want to keep lucky.
