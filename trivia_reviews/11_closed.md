# Trivia review 11 — Japanese read for sense, all 273 articles (2026-11-06)

Owner: 「読み物系で、意味わからん文章が目立つ」, with the Wang Xihou sentence as the example. The owner misread it as Wang being criticised; he was the critic. A sentence a native misreads is at fault, so it was rewritten, with the fact unchanged.

Three model readers went through every Japanese article cold and flagged sentences that had to be read twice. 356 fixes: split sentences, named subjects, unclear これ/それ, calques. I read all 356 pairs before applying. Brief and files: ~/langmap-work/ja_read.

Found on the way, and affecting all 19 languages:
- 62 section headings in 47 word and name stories sat under the wrong paragraph, mostly one paragraph late. Moved in the layout of each src file, by comparing heading and paragraph text. No heading moved in the older articles, which were not checked this way.
- wheel: "first recorded use is before about 3500 BC" reversed the argument. Now "No wheel is known from before about 3500 BC".
- kokugo: 私はコーヒーを飲む was said to have all three strata and has no kango. Now 私は毎日コーヒーを飲む. The zh copy had 饮む, a script-conversion error, now 飲む and listed in the guard.
- sentinelese: "the only word" contradicted liya and luwa in the same article. Now excepts the shouted words.

Japanese only: salt stray sentence cut; Hebrew 8,000 words now Biblical only; kanbun 形成 softened to the English "influenced"; chu-nom title and summary brought down to the English; タア語 and the Ledyard and Phagpa spellings unified.

Left: quoc-ngu numbers the level tone first in ja and sixth in en, and its tl- to tr- conclusion does not follow; rotokas lists the 2019 referendum under the late 20th century; go-on and pie are stronger in ja than en; 字喃 and シャニ go unexplained.
The other 17 languages did not get this read. Sentences that fail in Japanese because the English was compressed may fail there too. No human has read the fixes.
