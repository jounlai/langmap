# Trivia review 03 — 10 new Name Map and 10 new Time Map articles (2026-11-05)

Written in en and ja, source-checked, then translated into the other 17 languages. Every language had a read without the
English beside it by a reader who was not its translator. No native speaker has read them.
Name Map: peter-rock, joseph-pepe, catherine-kate-katya, margaret-pearl, michael-question, sophia-wisdom,
abdullah-servant-names, moses-musa, anthony-tony, noah-nuh. Time Map: persian-exact-year and nine others.
Dates and buttons were computed with timemap_calendars.js. Behind the Name is not cited: its robots.txt bars this client.
Sources are mostly Wikipedia and Wiktionary. Notes and doubts per language: ~/langmap-work/trivia3/native_notes.md.
