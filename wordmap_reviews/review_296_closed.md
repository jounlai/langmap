## Reviewer self-introduction (ペルソナ自己紹介)
I am Dr. Aïssata Konaré, a descriptive/historical linguist specializing in West African Niger-Congo languages outside Bantu, with fieldwork on Manding and Gur varieties. For this review I leaned on the standard reference corpus: Gérard Dumestre's *Grammaire fondamentale du bambara* and *Dictionnaire bambara–français*, together with Bird & Kanté for Jula/Bambara and Denis Creissels for Mandinka; Robert Carlson's *A Grammar of Supyire* (Mouton de Gruyter, 1994) for Senoufo Supyire; Claire Lefebvre & Anne-Marie Brousseau, *A Grammar of Fongbe* (2002) for Fon; William J. Samarin's *A Grammar of Sango* and *Basic Course in Sango* for Sango; Knut J. Olawsky's *A Grammar of Dagbani* and Tony Naden's Mabia (Gur) comparative work for Dagbani/Mampruli/Mooré; David Sapir's *A Grammatical Sketch of Diola-Fogny* (1965) for Jola-Fonyi; William Welmers on Kpelle; and SIL lexical materials for Baoulé, Bassa (Kru), Susu, and the Grassfields/Adamawa varieties. Tone transcriptions were checked against each source's citation conventions.
