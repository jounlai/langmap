Two sub-threshold observations are recorded for future verification but are **not** submitted as corrections, to respect the corpus's anti-fabrication standard (I can cast doubt but cannot supply a source-verified replacement):

- **`bfa` — star — `["toŋ","toŋ"]`:** Bari *toŋ* collides with the widespread Nilotic root *\*tʊŋ/tɔŋ* 'spear/horn' (cf. Dinka *tɔŋ* 'spear', *tuŋ* 'horn'). Eastern Nilotic 'star' normally carries the *-kir-* root (Maasai *ol-akira*, Turkana/Teso reflexes), so *toŋ* looks like a possible sense-confusion. I could not confirm the exact Bari 'star' lexeme in Spagnolo from memory, so I decline to invent a replacement — recommend checking Spagnolo (1960) before any change.
