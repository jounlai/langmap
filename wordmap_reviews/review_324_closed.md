## Issues found
No errors found — all cells verified correct.

I scrutinized the four cells most exposed to error and judged each acceptable rather than a defect: (a) the Beti pair `ewo`/`bum` uses the older mission (church) orthography in which circumflex **ô** = close /o/ and plain **o** = open /ɔ/, so `jôé` → /ʒóé/ and `ôtɛtɛ` → /ótɛtɛ/ are internally consistent, not vowel-quality errors; (b) `agq` "I" = `à` is defensible as the Aghem 1sg subject form in Hyman's analysis and I could not positively disconfirm it in favour of a 3sg reading; (c) the two southwestern "star" cells I could not verify against a primary source — `umb` `onyeleñgele` (ñg = /ŋɡ/) and `lue` `tanganyeka` — but neither is demonstrably a wrong-sense or wrong-script entry, so under the "flag genuine errors only" rule they stand. Segmental/prenasalization notation is coherent throughout (`ng` `ᵐbali`, `ng` `eðina` for orthographic *dh*, `xog` `erinɲa` for *nny*), and the pronoun senses are all singular informal as required (Makhuwa `miyo`/`weyo`, Oshiwambo `ame`/`ove`~`ngoye`, Herero `ami`/`ove`, Tonga `ime`/`iwe`, Lusoga `nze`/`gwe`).
