Iberian `bi` (2) matches the Ferrer i Jané / Orduña reconstruction of the Vasconic-looking numeral series. Nivkh Cyrillic (ӄ ӈ ӷ) and the palatal `ни/чи` pronouns are orthographically sound. Warao `manamo` (2) is correct. Pirahã `hoí` (rising tone) is the "larger-quantity/two" member of the pair, correctly distinguished from falling `hòí`, and its tone marking is internally consistent. Where I lacked citation-grade certainty (e.g. Hadza/Sandawe `tsʼamako`/`tsʼama` for 'star', Kusunda `dukhu`/`sam`, Yuchi `weʼyv`, Rotokas `oira`/`kepatu`), the given forms are consistent with the documented phonologies and I found no basis to overturn them; I decline to propose speculative replacements that would be applied to live data.
