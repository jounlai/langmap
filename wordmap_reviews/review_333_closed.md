- **ja `you` = あなた/anata** — not the *most* casual 2sg (cf. きみ/おまえ), but it is the neutral, non-honorific citation form and a genuine 2sg pronoun; acceptable as the standard-language representative alongside the informal dialect forms (あんた, な, わい, おめ).
