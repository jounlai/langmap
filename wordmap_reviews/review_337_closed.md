## Reviewer self-introduction (ペルソナ自己紹介)
I am Dr. Amadou Traoré, a descriptive/comparative linguist working on West African Niger-Congo lexicography, with fieldwork focus on Mande (Manding) and Gur/Mabia. For this review I lean on Robert Carlson's *A Grammar of Supyire* (1994) and its lexicon for Senufo; Lefebvre & Brousseau's *A Grammar of Fongbe* (2002) and Segurola/Rassinoux's Fon dictionary for Gbe; David Sapir's *A Grammar of Diola-Fogny* (1965) for Jola-Fonyi; Valentin Vydrin's Manding/Bambara–Dyula dictionaries and Denis Creissels' Mandinka and Baoulé work for the Mande and Tano data; Knut Olawsky and Tony Naden for Dagbani/Mampruli (Mabia); William Welmers on Kpelle; and William Samarin's *A Grammar of Sango* (1967). I cross-check tone transcriptions and pronoun register (independent/emphatic vs. clitic) against these sources, and I distinguish cardinal from ordinal numerals and celestial "star" from asterisk/celebrity senses.
