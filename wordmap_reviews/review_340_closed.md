## Issues found

### 1. `chp` — name — "little" confused with "name"
- **File:** `words/name.js` — code `chp`
- **Current:** ["yází","jází"]
- **Expected:** ["-zí","zí"]
- **Why:** In Chipewyan (Dëne Sųłiné), *yázi* / *-yází* is the diminutive "little, small, junior" (cognate with Navajo *-yázhí* "little one"), not "name." The noun "name" is the bound root *-zí* (citation *bezí* "his/her name," *uzí* "its name"), directly parallel to this dataset's own Navajo `-zhiʼ` [ʒìʔ] and Carrier `uzi`. The current cell has substituted the "small" word for "name." Corrected to the bound noun root *-zí* [zí].
