## Issues found

No errors found — all cells verified correct.

The dataset in this domain is unusually clean and the fine-grained sociophonetic distinctions are handled accurately. Points I specifically confirmed rather than corrected:
- **Central American voseo** (es_cr, es_hn, es_ni, es_sv, es_gt, es_py) correctly show `vos` [bos] as the informal 2sg, while Caribbean/tuteo areas (es_pr, es_do, es_ve, es_pa) and Andean es_bo/es_ec correctly show `tú` [tu]. Costa Rican/Guatemalan retention of coda [s] vs. Caribbean aspiration/deletion is right.
- **Caribbean coda /s/** is handled with real precision: Dominican *dos* = [do] (final deletion) but *estrella* = [ehˈtɾeʝa] (internal preconsonantal aspiration); Panama/Puerto Rico *dos* = [doh] (aspiration). These are the attested, differentiated outcomes, not a uniform template.
- **Rioplatense** es_uy correctly isolates *šeísmo*: `yo` [ʃo], `estrella` [esˈtɾeʃa].
- **es_eu** is internally consistent as a *distinción* representative: `yo` [ʝo] (⟨y⟩ is always /ʝ/) alongside `estrella` [esˈtɾeʎa] (⟨ll⟩ = /ʎ/).
- **Galician** `ti` [ˈti] is the correct Galician tonic subject pronoun (distinct from Portuguese *tu*), and `nome` [ˈnɔme] correctly carries the open /ɔ/.
- **Sicilian** `stidda` [ˈstɪɖːa] / `dui` [ˈduɪ]: the retroflex geminate ⟨ɖː⟩ from Latin -ll- is correct, and the [ɪ]/[ʊ] realizations of high vowels in checked syllables are a documented Sicilian feature (not an Italianising error), so I let them stand.
- **Norman** (dgr, fra_jer) `jé` [ʒe] and `êtaile` [etɛl] are the correct Jèrriais/Guernésiais lexemes for 'I' and 'star'.
- **pt_br** `você` [voˈse] is the appropriate informal 2sg for Brazil; final-vowel raising (`nome` [ˈnomi], `estrela` [isˈtɾelɐ]) is right.
- **fr_qc** affrication (`tu` [tsy]) and back [ɑ] in `étoile` [etwɑl] are correct Québec features; **fr_ci** `je` [ʒe] reflects the well-documented Ivorian tense-vowel/schwa-loss pattern and is defensible against the [ʒə] of the other French varieties.
