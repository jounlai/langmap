ISN pronouns are realized by indexic pointing (INDEX-1 for "I", INDEX-2 for "you"), the numeral "two" by a two-finger handshape, and "name"/"star" by lexical signs specified in handshape, location, movement and orientation — none of which map onto the [native-orthography, broad-IPA] cells this dataset uses for spoken languages.
