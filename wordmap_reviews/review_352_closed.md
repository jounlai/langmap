## Issues found
### 1. `new` — star — wrong form (looks copied from "two")
- **File:** `words/new.js` — code `new`
- **Current:** ["नगु","nagu"]
- **Expected:** ["नखः","nakʰaː"]
- **Why:** "nagu" is not the Newari (Nepal Bhāṣā) word for a celestial star. The native Kathmandu-Newari lexeme is नखः /nakʰaː/ (Kölver & Shresthacarya, *A Dictionary of Contemporary Newari*; also Hale & Shrestha). The listed नगु nagu is almost certainly a fill/copy error from the adjacent numeral "two" निगु nigu (the two forms differ only in the first-syllable vowel na~ni), which is independently and correctly given in this same row. The final syllable of the true star word is aspirated -khaḥ, not -gu.
