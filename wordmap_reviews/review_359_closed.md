## Issues found
### 1. `ctu` — i — reduced clitic given where full independent pronoun is expected
- **File:** `words/i.js` — code `ctu`
- **Current:** ["joñ","hoɲ"]
- **Expected:** ["joñoñ","hoɲoɲ"]
- **Why:** The Chol first-person independent/predicative pronoun is **joñoñ** (1sg, "I / I am"), built on the -oñ set-B suffix, exactly parallel to the 2sg **jatyet** already used in the `you` cell (Vázquez Álvarez 2011, ch. on pronouns; Aulie & Aulie, *Diccionario Ch'ol*). Bare "joñ" is not the citation form of the pronoun and creates an asymmetry with the full 2sg **jatyet** in the same row; the sister language Tabasco Chontal (chf) is correctly given as the matched full pair **noʼon / neʼet**, confirming the full-form convention. The 1sg should therefore be the full **joñoñ** /hoɲoɲ/.
