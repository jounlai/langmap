## Issues found
I checked every cell on three axes: (1) **sense** — that "i"/"you" are singular 1sg/2sg nominative (not plural, dual, or honorific), "two" is the cardinal numeral (not an ordinal or a "a couple/pair" verb), and "name"/"star" are the correct common nouns (no proper names, celebrity/asterisk intrusions); (2) **script** — standard practical orthography for each community, including the Damin ceremonial-register click notation (⟨!⟩ and ⟨*⟩ per Hale); (3) **IPA** — segmental plausibility and the peripheral/laminal/retroflex contrasts typical of the family.

Results of that pass:
- All 17 "i" forms are genuine 1sg nominatives (ngaja, ngayu, ngaju, ngayulu, ayenge, ngayi, ay, ngai, ngany, ngaya, ngada, n!aa, ngayu). No plural/dual intrusions.
- All 17 "you" forms are 2sg singular (nginda, nyuntu, unte, nyini, inag, nhina, noonook, ngindu, nyingki, n!uu, nyingka, nyundu). No honorific or plural (nyurra-type) contamination.
- All "two" forms are the cardinal numeral (bulgany, kujarra/kutjara(rra), jirrama, atherre, wuthada, uchir, arlpili, koodjal, bula, kiyarrng(ka), gudhirra, r!ii). No ordinals.
- "name" and "star" forms are common nouns with family-plausible shapes; the Wati "yini/ini" for name, Arrernte "arritnye", Warlpiri "yirdi", Lardil "wangalk", and Damin "m!ii"/"l*i" all check out against the cited sources.

No cell met the bar for correction — no wrong-sense forms, no non-standard script, and no IPA transcription implausible enough to override given the orthographic conventions of each source.
