The 1sg and 2sg forms are all the correct informal subject pronouns (Galician tonic *ti*; Venetian/Ligurian/Tabarchino *mi/ti*; Arpitan's internally consistent clitic series *je/te*; Brazilian *você* as the genuine informal 2sg address, distinct from European/African *tu*). The numeral, "name," and "star" cells are all the right senses and native orthographies (Sicilian *stidda* [ˈstɪɖːa] with correct retroflex; Romanian *stea* [ste̯a]; Occitan *estèla* [esˈtɛlɔ] with Languedocien final-a lowering). Diatopic phonology checks out: Rioplatense sheísmo (es_uy *yo* [ʃo] / *estrella* [esˈtɾeʃa]), Caribbean aspiration/deletion (es_pa/pr/ve [doh], es_do [do]), Valencian affricate *jo* [ˈd͡ʒɔ], and Québec *tu* [tsy]. The few marked cells (Ivorian *je* [ʒe]; non-aspirated /s/ in Central-American voseo varieties; Genoese *nomme* [ˈnomme]) are defensible dialect or broad-transcription conventions rather than errors.
