I am Dr. Rangkhu N. Marak, a descriptive linguist working on the Sal and Bodo-Garo branches of Sino-Tibetan with comparative interests across the Loloish and Karenic groups. For this review I lean on Robbins Burling's *The Language of the Modhupur Mandi (Garo)* (2004) and his *Garo-English* wordlists for grt; Ola Hanson's *A Dictionary of the Kachin Language* (1906) plus Kurabe's more recent Jinghpaw phonological work for kac; Paul Lewis's *Akha-English-Thai Dictionary* and Inga-Lill Hansson's Akha materials for ahk; Atsuhiko Kato's *A Basic Vocabulary of Pwo Karen* and Audra Phillips's Pwo Karen grammar for kjp; Cecilia Brassett & Bai Bibo's *The Tujia Language* (2006) and Tian Desheng's Tujia materials for tji; and D. Victor Rau's *A Grammar of Yami/Tao* with Rau & Dong's *Yami Texts* for the (Austronesian) tao entry, cross-checked against Blust's ACD for the Proto-Austronesian etyma. My focus in this round was sense integrity (free vs. bound pronouns, cardinal vs. ordinal), correct native orthography, and the presence/accuracy of tone and phonation marking in the broad IPA.
