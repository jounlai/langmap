## Issues found

No errors found — all cells verified correct.

All 1sg forms are genuine independent subject pronouns (Finnic *minä/mie/miä/mina*, Saami *mun/mån/manne/mon*, Permic *me/mon*, Mari *mый/мӹнь*, Mordvin *мон*, Samoyedic *мань/модь/мəнə/ман*, Ob-Ugric *ма/ам*); no possessive prefixes or emphatic compounds intruded. All 2sg forms are the informal register (Hungarian *te*, not honorific *ön/maga*; Finnish *sinä*, not plural *te*). All "two" cells are the free cardinal (Hungarian correctly shows independent *kettő*, not attributive *két*, and not an ordinal). All "name" and "star" cells are common nouns in the right sense (Hungarian *csillag* verified as the primary celestial noun, not the asterisk/celebrity reading). Native orthographies (Cyrillic vs. Latin, Saami diacritics *á/å/õ/â/ˊ*, Kildin macron/soft-sign, Livonian *ā/ḑ*, Võro *ś*) and the broad IPA (including *d→/t/* devoicing in North/Lule/South Saami, *дз→/dʑ/* in Komi *кодзув*, Mari central *ы/ӹ → /ɨ/,/ɘ/*) are all defensible. The two mis-domained non-Uralic rows (squ, tsi) are outside my family but their word cells are internally consistent with Kuipers and Dunn, so no word-cell correction is warranted.
