# Review 605 — gloss check of the ancient rows (2026-11-03)

Every cell in 45 attested historical rows (Latin, Ancient Greek, Sanskrit, Old
English … Phoenician; proto-languages left out) was looked up on en.wiktionary
in the row's own language section, and its definition lines were matched
against the concept (bat → "bat", poop → "excrement|dung|…").
Tools: ~/langmap-work/anc1103/collect.js, fetch.js, gloss.js.

3,258 cells: 1,938 glossed as the concept, 107 not, 1,211 with no entry
(Sanskrit cells are nominatives while Wiktionary lists stems; Sogdian,
Tocharian, Old Javanese, Middle Persian and Classical Persian are thin there).

The 107 mismatches, read by hand:
- ~90 are form-of or alternative-spelling entries (Sanskrit nominatives,
  Latin infinitives, Middle English spelling variants) whose lemma is the
  concept. No change.
- Homographs where Wiktionary lists only the other word: Old High German
  hunt and Old Saxon hund 'hundred' (also 'dog'), Middle Korean 바·회 'wheel'
  (also 'rock'; 訓蒙字會 輪 바회 륜), Avestan nauua 'new' (also 'nine'), Old
  Turkic balïq 'fish' (also 'town'), Phoenician ʾš 'fire', ʾdm 'red', bt
  'daughter' (unvocalised script). No change.
- Left open, no source either way: Phoenician 𐤔𐤕 'drink' (Wiktionary has only
  'year'), Old Persian 𐎠𐎫𐎼 ātar 'fire' (the spelling is also antar 'inside'),
  Sumerian 𒄵 giriš 'butterfly'.

No cell changed. Next step would be the 1,211 no-entry cells, which need
the row's own dictionaries (Monier-Williams, CAL, Steingass…), not Wiktionary.
