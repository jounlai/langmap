Held:
- rwk dog: only plural makite is attested; a singular ikite would be built, and ikite is 'oath'.
- swb love mahaba: absence from the NT does not show the everyday word is wrong.
- vai heart ꕘꕞ: the NT word may be 'liver' in its figurative heart sense (ASJP liver fa). This
  atlas means the organ, so a verse about the inner heart does not settle it.
- mgo we mbǎ (John 4:22): one unlabelled form would need route unknown, and that route may only shrink, so the cell stays empty.
- vai red: kpou is Migeod's Mende word, so the cell is wrong, but the IPA of the NT's ꕧꕌ is not
  known (ꕌ may only mark length).

Method limit: an NT shows the translator's register. Love cells are the weakest evidence here.
