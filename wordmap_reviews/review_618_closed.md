## mic: one orthography
The row mixed g spellings (18 cells) and k spellings (25), and the description named Smith-Francis.
Its only named dictionary, Mi'gmaq Online, is the Listuguj g spelling and matches 53 of 87 cells.
26 cells moved to Listuguj forms printed there, with the entry pages' pronunciation guides correcting
7 IPA strings (good, milk, you, foot, heart, sun, stone). Word-level choices: house wenjiʼguom (the
dictionary's 'house'; wikuom's word is wigwom 'wigwam, camp'), love gesaluet ('he/she loves', the
same stem; gesalg is not printed), hello gweʼ (Mi'gmaq Wiki Gwe'; Mi'gmaq Online has only pusu'l;
IPA not printed). The foot note's Francis-Smith clause and blue's orthography caveat were removed.
The description now names both orthographies and says the map follows Listuguj, in all 9 versions.
