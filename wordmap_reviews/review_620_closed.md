# Review 620 — Lezgic х, and the fifth group of rows (2026-11-04)

## х in Lezgic rows
Kassian's list prints plain х as χ in every Cyrillic form checked, and хь as x. The rows wrote plain х as
x in many cells. 35 cells now read χ in tab, rut, bdk, udi, lez, aqc: about half printed by Kassian,
the rest by the letter (including thanks, wheel, wine, the loans). bdk egg stays x: Kassian has кусхьуд.
