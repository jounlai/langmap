# Review 635 — Pacific rows from public-domain dictionaries (2026-11-05)

ho (Police Motu 1961), meu (Lawes 1896), sm (Pratt 1893), fj (Hazlewood 1890), rap (Churchill 1912), ty (Davies 1851), haw (Andrews 1865). 4 fixes, 26 new cells.
Fixes: ho chocolate sogeledi; meu cat pose; meu honey labolabo bata ranuna (bata is wax, bada big); sm heart fatu (Pratt: the organ, not the affections).
Rejected: forms likely missing length or glottal in old books (sm ponaata, ty piao, tui, haw mauliawa), sm goosebumps veli, sm turkey, haw dragonfly (example only), pis tomato, rap older brother, thumb.
Kept: sm hiccup lona, whose note cites the newer Milner dictionary. Open: meu hello namo gida; rap little finger ko manara may be a slip for manava.
