# Review 668 — Luwian row into hieroglyphs (2026-11-06)

Owner: the xlu row mixed hieroglyphs and romanization; unify in hieroglyphs where possible.

Rule: a cell turns into hieroglyphs only when a real spelling of that word was found. No cuneiform transliteration was converted by rule. Source: ACLT (luwian.web-corpora.net, 2,506 sentences, 266 documents), one spelling per cell, cited in the cell note; Wiktionary as a cross-check.

Converted 20: daughter dog drink earth eat eye five foot four head heart horse love mother mountain person stone three wine you. The IPA now follows the spelled form, as father and house already did, so several cells are inflected (dog is plural, eat is an infinitive). five is the numeral sign alone, so its IPA stays the cuneiform reading. earth and mother show the hieroglyphic stems takami-, annatti-, not the cuneiform tiyammi-, anni-.

Stay in transliteration, 10: blood bone ear fire honey mouth new tongue water we. ACLT has no spelled form. nawa- there means great-great-grandson, not new. we has a-za5-za, and the sign table has no za5.

Sign fixes in 3 older cells: hand sà and tara/i (A104A, A389, was A104, A388), name lá/í and ma (A175, A110A, was A172, A110), moon ma. Hand and name now cite ACLT.

Trap: the first sign table was keyed by the last word of each label, so compounds overwrote plain logograms: SCALPRUM gave CAPERE+SCALPRUM and TERRA gave (DEUS)VIA+TERRA. Rebuilt with full labels (~/langmap-work/xlu1106/signs2.json) before applying. Sign values rest on Wikipedia's table, checked against Wiktionary spellings where a sign appears there. Ligatures such as ha+ra/i are written as two signs. Not checked against photographs or Hawkins' CHLI.
