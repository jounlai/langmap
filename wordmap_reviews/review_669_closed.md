# Review 669 — China in Inner Mongolia (closed 2026-11-08)

Source: a reader comment (WL Chan). Turkic languages mostly say Khitai, but in China the name is politically sensitive, so Uyghurs and Kazakhs there say Junggo; Mongols in Inner Mongolia officially say Dumdadu ulus ᠳᠤᠮᠳᠠᠳᠤ ᠤᠯᠤᠰ, "middle country".

- ug: already جۇڭگو Junggo, route zhongguo. No change.
- kk: the row is Kazakhstan Kazakh, so Қытай stays. There is no China Kazakh row.
- mn: Хятад stays; its note already gives Дундад улс as the form used in China.
- mn_cn: had no China cell. Added ᠳᠤᠮᠳᠠᠳᠤ ᠤᠯᠤᠰ [tʊmtɑt ʊls], route zhongguo (a calque, as Manchu dulimbai gurun). Script form from Wiktionary Дундад улс; IPA in the row's Chakhar style is mine.

## Follow-up, same thread: Tibetan རྒྱ་ནག

A second comment: Tibetan has another traditional name, རྒྱ་ནག rgya nag, "black China".

- bo: had no China cell. Added རྒྱ་ནག [ca˩˧ naʔ˥˧], route other, the native "black expanse" name paired with རྒྱ་གར "white expanse" for India; the note gives ཀྲུང་གོ krung go (from Mandarin) as the official name in China. Wiktionary རྒྱ་ནག, Lhasa /ɟa˩˨.na˥˨/; IPA in the row's style.
- The China article counted 90 middle-country cells and 11 others; with mn_cn and bo they are 91 and 12. Both numbers fixed in all 19 languages, and the article now names Tibetan beside Sikkimese for the "black expanse". Translations are mine, not read by natives.
