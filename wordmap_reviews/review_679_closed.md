2026-11-09. Stress-mark policy (owner 2026-07-21): polysyllabic single words carry ˈ, monosyllables and phrases do not; only English had been normalised. Rows where 40% or more of the polysyllabic single-word cells already carry ˈ (and not all) are rows whose own convention is to mark stress. In those rows, a polysyllabic cell without ˈ got the mark only when its surface is a Wiktionary headword whose IPA marks stress, with the same syllable count and no non-syllabic vowels; the mark goes before the onset of the same syllable (~/langmap-work/stress1109/plan.py).
