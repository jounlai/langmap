## Route rule
A picture (stick, key, pen, finger) wins over a plain label (memory, drive, flash, usb); among plain labels the head noun decides.
- he דיסק און קי (DiskOnKey) put under key ("disk on a key ring"); could be argued as drive.
- usb = the device called by its plug's name: vi USB ("u ét bê", vi WP), ur یو ایس بی (ur WP), mk уесбе (Wiktionary).

## Doubts
- ta விரலி: Tamil Wikipedia's own word (from விரல் "finger"); speakers may say பென் டிரைவ். Check before publishing.
- id diska lepas and ms pemacu kilat are the official calques (Wiktionary entry / WP title); speech often says flashdisk / pendrive.
- sw diski mweko: WP title + Wiktionary; everyday Swahili may just say "flash".
- en_us flash drive: no US-labelled source; chosen from the Wiktionary main entry and the article title.
- ka ფლეშკა: Wiktionary marks it informal/proscribed; IPA has f, which the ka row lacks (loan sound, kept).
- Guard flags left in: nl yɛsˈbeːˌstɪk and fr kle yɛsbe ("y in IPA": the letter U is [y]); cy fɔŋ ɡoːv (ŋ not in row; Wiktionary nasal assimilation).
- ja USBメモリ and ko USB 메모리 mix Latin letters with kana/Hangul (written that way); ko WP notes it is often just "USB".
- de USB-Stick IPA follows Wiktionary [stɪk]; [ʃtɪk] is also heard.
