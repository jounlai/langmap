## Doubts
- Many languages use English jet lag in speech while Wikipedia titles carry a coinage (id mabuk pascaterbang, fa پرواززدگی, ta, sc, bjn, uk Десинхроноз, pl Zespół nagłej zmiany strefy czasowej, ca Trastorn d'horari); those rows were skipped rather than filled with the coinage, and the borrowed form was filled only where a pronunciation source exists. So `jetlag` is under-counted in the map (it, nl, da, no, pl, tr, vi, hr, sr all use the loan but lack IPA sources).
- `timediff` merges 'time difference' (時差ぼけ, 时差, 시차, aikaerorasitus) with 'time zone' (pásmová nemoc, horzonozo) and French décalage horaire 'time offset'.
- zh/zh_tw/yue use the noun 時差/时差, whose entry has the sense 'jet lag'; 倒时差 is the verb 'to get over jet lag' (named in the definition, not mapped). ko 시차 likewise (Wiktionary: 'time difference or jetlag'); the Korean Wikipedia title is 시차증.
- he יעפת (WP title, coined from the roots of 'tired' and 'fly') is the only `other`; ג׳ט לג is also in the translation table. IPA jaˈefet follows the row (no ʕ/ʔ for ע).
- Loan IPA from other Wiktionaries: de ˈdʒɛtlɛk (de.wiktionary), sv ˈjɛtlɛk (sv.wiktionary gives ˈ(dz)jɛtlɛk; the optional onset dropped), ru dʐʐɨtˈɫak (en.wiktionary, gemination kept as given). hu ˈdʒɛtlɛɡ from hu WP's "kiejtése: dzsetleg". el dzet laɡ, hy dʒetʰˈlɑɡ, hi dʒeːʈ lɛːɡ, bn dʒeʈ læɡ, cs, sk, eo are read off regular spelling (WP titles).
