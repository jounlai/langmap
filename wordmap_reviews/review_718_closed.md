# Review 718 — Hokkien and Teochew "left" (2026-11-12)

The Sinitic fill (review 711) gave nan_ph / nan_pn 倒 tò from Wiktionary's dialect table for 左, which lists 倒 for Xiamen, Quanzhou,
Zhangzhou, Taipei, Kaohsiung, Tainan, Chaozhou and Shantou. The older nan, nan_xm, nan_qz, nan_zz, nan_te cells held the literary 左.
Changed to 倒 with each row's own 陰去 tone: nan to˩˩, nan_xm to˨˩, nan_zz to˨˩, nan_qz tɤ˦˩, nan_te to˨˩˧ (major-word rule).
