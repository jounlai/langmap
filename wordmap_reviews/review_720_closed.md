# Review 720 — yellow: Persian زرد on the yellow-green root (2026-11-12)

Open since 2026-11-12: the same Iranian word for yellow sat on two routes — fa, prs, tg (and the loan sd) on "gold", ku, ckb, bal,
zza on "ghel" (the old root for yellow and green). Wiktionary derives Persian زرد from Middle Persian zard, Proto-Iranian *zr̥Hdás,
from PIE *ǵʰelh₃- 'green, yellow'; زر 'gold' is a relative from the same root, not its source. Policy decided: a word goes on the route
of its own etymology; a relative's meaning is a note. fa, prs, tg, sd → ghel (gold route now Vietnamese vàng and the like).
