# Word Map review 754 — new stage row: Middle Vietnamese (vi_mid) (2026-11-17)

Era split. 17c, from Alexandre de Rhodes, Dictionarium Annamiticum Lusitanum et Latinum (Rome 1651), read column by column
from the Commons scan (columns cited per cell in ~/langmap-work/era1117/vi_mid). 113 cells; spelling as printed (bl, tl,
ꞗ, ᷃; long ſ → s, initial u → v); IPA after Gregerson 1969, tone letters as the vi row. Pin Kẻ Chợ, nudged off vi's Hanoi.
Clusters: moon mặt blang, sun mặt blời, egg tlứng, hundred tlăm, white tláng, tea tlà; ꞗua king, ꞗợ wife. Different
words: father cha, sea bể, sugar đàng cát, husband chào᷃, spider rện, rainbow sổ blời, island núi nước, wine nước blái nho,
tobacco thuốc lào, Korea cao li, Japan nhịt bổn, you mầy, thanks cám ơn.
Left empty: China (only contemptuous nước ngô / dynasty name đại minh), we (no inclusive/exclusive statement — memory
wordmap-we-column-rule), hello. Hindi name मध्यकालीन वियतनामी (मध्य वियतनामी is Central Vietnamese).
Genuine shared forms: xanh blue/green (grue), vàng gold/yellow.
HOLD: blue xanh (de Rhodes glosses it "verde" but uses it of the sky); right mặt; you mầy is said to inferiors.
