# Word Map review 756 — Sibe (sjo) written in Sibe letters and spoken Sibe (2026-11-17)

Owner question: 「満州語とシベ語は綴りが一緒？」 Answer: mostly the same letters, different characters. Written Sibe keeps
the Manchu spelling of most words (niyalma, bithe, morin, -mbi verbs), but the 1947 reform changed the shapes of i, f, k,
ng, j, r, which Unicode encodes as separate Sibe characters (i U+185E, k U+1863, f U+186B, j U+186A, ng U+1862, r U+1837
vs Manchu U+1873, U+1874, U+1876, U+1835, U+1829, U+1875) — abkai.net/blog/?p=107, Wikipedia "Xibe language", every Xibe
entry in Wiktionary. Real spelling differences are few (wi/wo/wu, negative =rku for =raku). The spoken language differs a
lot from the Manchu reading (Li Shulan & Zhong Qian 1986): final vowels drop, š > s, niyalma is said nɑn.
