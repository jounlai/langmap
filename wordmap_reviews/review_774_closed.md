Closed 2026-11-18. Five draft-only agents worked through 38 rows. 271 cells were applied across 23 rows.

- **Wolof:** the Wolof Bible (ebible wolmbs).
- **Also:** Wiktionary entries that carry Tifinagh spellings (Tashelhit and Tarifit).

## Accepted conventions
- The tzm Tifinagh is converted 1:1 from Taifi's transcription by the IRCAM letter table, which is the row's own convention.
- dje husband: the ny in the source is written ɲ, matching the row's spelling.

## Skipped at apply
- **ar_ma** thumb and little finger: each spelling joins two attested words, and no source prints the phrase.
- **ses** elephant: the form is inferred from "elephant tooth" (ivory).
- **tzm** tomorrow: the route was unclear. It would be morning by resemblance to Tashelhit, but Taifi files the word under "day".

## Held, no source in the row's own spelling
- **dzg:** its only source is Webonary, which is barred.
- **dyo, khq, fia, dds, mnk, mos, mwk and fvr:** no source in the row's spelling.
- **tmh:** the row is written in Neo-Tifinagh, and no source prints these words in that script.
- **arq:** the IPA would have to be derived by hand.
