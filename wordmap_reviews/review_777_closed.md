# Review 777 — ancient rally A09 (classical American languages, Old Nubian, Damin)

Closed 2026-11-18. The rows are emy, nci, qwc, tpn, chb, onw and lbz_damin. 109 cells were applied.

## Sources
- **Classic Maya:** Boot 2009 and Kettunen & Helmke 2004.
- **Classical Nahuatl:** Molina 1571 via GDN.
- **Classical Quechua:** the 1586 Lima Lexicon and González Holguín 1608.
- **Tupinambá:** the Vocabulário na Língua Brasílica, Navarro 2013 and Wiktionary.
- **Muisca:** Uricoechea 1871 and Lugo 1619.
- **Old Nubian:** Griffith 1913 and Smagina 2017.
- **Damin:** Hale & Nash 1997. The row already holds every published root that matches a concept on the map.

## Native words found
- **Maize:** sara (Quechua), ixim (Maya).
- **Chocolate:** kakaw (Maya), chocolātl (Nahuatl).
- **Tobacco:** sayri, kʼuuhtz, petyma, hosca.
- **Chili:** uchu, quibsa, kyʼynha.
- **Pineapple:** naná (Tupi, the source of ananás), achupalla.

## Stand-ins and colonial words (with reader notes)
- **Lion:** the puma in Quechua, Maya and Muisca.
- **Wine:** pulque (chih) in Classic Maya.
- **Potato:** the sweet potato in Nahuatl and Tupi.
- **Horse:** kawallu, from caballo.
- **Silk:** Molina's "worm thread".

## Skipped at apply
- tpn grandmother, cotton, octopus, rainbow, hiccup and island: in each, the î was restored from OCR, which drops it.
- tpn money itaîuba: it repeats the gold word, but carries the silver route.
