# Review 778 — ancient rally A10 (proto-languages)

Closed 2026-11-18. Four draft-only agents covered 23 proto rows. Protos get no stand-ins, no modern concepts and no reconstructions of our own. 166 cells were applied, each one a form printed in a published source.

## Sources
- Pittayaporn 2009 (Proto-Tai).
- Kaufman 2003 (Proto-Mayan).
- Ratliff 2010 (Proto-Hmong-Mien, level-1 forms only).
- STEDT (Proto-Tibeto-Burman).
- ACD CLDF (Proto-Austronesian).
- Kogan 2011, via Wiktionary (Proto-Semitic).
- Wiktionary Reconstruction pages for the other rows.

## Notable finds
- Proto-Japonic *namita "tear", *nonsi "rainbow" and *kuntira "whale".
- p_jpk had been missing 15 core Swadesh items.
- Proto-Mayan *juun winaq "twenty", literally "one person".
- Proto-Hmong-Mien money and thousand are loans from Chinese 錢 and 千.

## Skipped at apply
- **pafa** dog and tree: these "—" cells are deliberate, and Proto-Afroasiatic is too contested to override them.
- **p_aav** bear: the only source is a Proto-Mon-Khmer form.
- **psem** tear and thumb: the forms have unresolved V vowels.
- **ptai** tomorrow: the row drops presyllables, and the stripped form is not printed anywhere.

## Guards
- **Fixed:** psem hand IPA yad → jad. The glide is written j, as in the new *yamīn- cell.
- **Proto-leak locks:** ojp tear and thousand are inherited; xng thumb is circular, since Middle Mongol is the basis of Proto-Mongolic.
- **Genuine duplicates accepted:**
  - green = yellow in pafa, pura and p_dra.
  - blue = green in p_ryu and p_jpk.
  - *ti in p_jpn and p_jpk (blood, milk, thousand) and in p_ryu (blood, milk).
  - paus *qasawa for both husband and wife.
  - pkar husband = person, p_jpk one = person.
