# Review 782 — gap rally R15 (Latin America: national varieties and creoles)

Closed 2026-11-18. The national Spanish and Portuguese variety rows had almost no gaps. The few left (bubble tea, jellyfish, USB stick) have no country-labelled source, and the Diccionario de americanismos does not cover them. So the fill went to the creoles: 183 new cells and 7 corrections.

## Sources
- **Bibles:** the wol.jw.org NWT and its daily texts (for weekday names) in Aukan, Guianese Creole, Sranan and Papiamentu; the ebible NTs for djk, srn and acf.
- **Dictionaries and word lists:** WOLD Saramaccan (with tones), the 2001 Kwéyòl Dictionary (cited in the acf row's meta), the Kaseko lexicon (HTML page only) and Freelang Papiamento.

## Existing cells corrected
- **srm chili and potato:** in the IPA, ë → ɛ.
- **acf rainbow:** in the IPA, ɑ̃ → ã.
- **acf bear bè:** removed. The Kwéyòl Dictionary has bè only as "butter", and this unsourced cell dated from Round 28.
- **gcr good and hello:** in the IPA, õ → ɔ̃, as in the rest of the row.
- **pap Sunday:** diadomingu → djadumingu, per Wiktionary and the JW Curaçao daily text.
- **pap Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday:** in the IPA, dj → dʒ, and Friday also gains its ɛ.

## Skipped at apply
- **acf and gcr grandmother and uncle:** in each case one unlabelled form shows no split, so it cannot be given the "one" route.
