## Guards
These are genuine Mesoamerican homonyms, accepted:
- jun / un / huuj / xapa: book and paper; jun is also "one".
- kan / chan: four and snake.
- takʼin: iron and money.
- qʼeq: black and soap. The word refers to the old black tallow soap.
- mari / xheelaʼ / itʼok: one word for both husband and wife.
