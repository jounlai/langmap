# Review 785 — cheers sweep (owner 2026-11-18: 「cheersが少ないのがちょっともったいない。もっと頑張って。」)

Closed 2026-11-18. Before the sweep, 206 rows had a cheers cell. Five draft-only agents took the 300 best-filled rows that were missing one and searched each for its everyday toast. Then FineWeb-2 web text was checked for English-loan toasts in South Asian scripts. 38 cells were applied.

## How toasts were sourced
- **Dictionary entries** that gloss the word as a toast:
  - mt saħħa, an salut, csb próst, tn pula.
  - ckb نۆش: Kurdish Wiktionary says it is said "when glasses clink".
  - ty manuia (Lemaître).
  - haw ʻōkole maluna (Pukui-Elbert, "bottoms up").
- **Phrasebooks:** the Wikivoyage Bars/Drinking sections, for bn, rw, pam and others.
- **Articles:** Wikipedia "Toast (honor)" and articles on drinking culture: bbc lissoi, ceb/war tagay, be будзьма.
- **Usage in FineWeb-2 web text:** contexts were read for te చీర్స్ (92 sites), ne चियर्स (50), kn ಚಿಯರ್ಸ್, gu ચિયર્સ, mr चियर्स and pa ਚੀਅਰਜ਼. Each was seen said as glasses or pegs are raised. All take route other, like hi चियर्स.

## Rejected
- **ur چیئرز:** in FineWeb it almost always means "chairs".
- **ory ଚିୟର୍ସ:** the hits are sports cheering.
- **ms "yam seng":** the hits are mostly "ayam sengaja", so there was no usable evidence.
- **Skipped at apply:**
  - pap: only the Aruban spelling "salud" is attested; the row writes Curaçao.
  - jam: the toast is attested only in English spelling, not in the row's orthography.
  - nso: the IPA was a guess.
  - sco: the IPA was adapted from Gaelic.
- **Omniglot:** its robots.txt bars ClaudeBot, so it was not used. Several rows had Omniglot as their only lead.

## Guards
- tn pula is both cheers and rain. This is a genuine double meaning: Botswana's toast, motto and currency are all "rain".
- The owner parity budget for cheers went from 32 to 34. The new parent cells expose child variety rows that have no toast source of their own.
