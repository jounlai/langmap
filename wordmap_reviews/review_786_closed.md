# Review 786 — gap rally R18: North American Indigenous rows and constructed languages

Closed 2026-11-18. Five draft-only agents worked through the North American Indigenous rows, plus Greenlandic and the constructed languages (Klingon, Lojban, Toki Pona, Interlingua). They looked for cells that "should be there but aren't". 419 cells were applied across 40 rows.

## Biggest fills
- **Rows with the most new cells:** tlh 36, hai 35, cr 34, tsi 33, arp 29, ia 23, esu 23, abe 23, mic 22, jbo 22, chr 17, oj 16, iu 16, chy 14, tok 10.
- **Sources:**
  - Klingon, Lojban (jbovlaste), Toki Pona and Interlingua: each language's own dictionary.
  - Sm'algyax: the UNBC Living Legacy Talking Dictionary.
  - Greenlandic: ordbog.gl.
  - Other rows: Wiktionary, community dictionaries and NT verse pairs.
- **Cells that share one word, each checked:**
  - Cherokee ᎧᎹᎹ is both butterfly and elephant; the elephant is named for its flapping ears.
  - Sm'algyax naks means both husband and wife (route spouse).
  - Several rows use the same word for book and paper.
  - Klingon sud covers yellow, green and blue.
  - Toki Pona pan covers bread, maize and rice.

## Corrected or removed
- **blc butter kʼucani:** removed. The word means a butter clam.
- **chr honey ᏩᏚᎵᏏ:** removed. The word means "bee".
- **lkt chocolate:** IPA corrected.
- **esu king ussʼutali:** the apostrophe was normalised to ʼ, the character used in the rest of the row.

## Skipped at apply
- **All 13 moh (Mohawk) drafts:** the row's IPA convention could not be matched from the sources.
- **kl lion løveq:** a Danish spelling with an IPA nobody printed.
- **kl woof.**
- **tsi lion and tsi monkey:** the glosses are likely the cougar and a forest being, not a lion or a monkey.

## Guards
- **Owner parity budget:** kiss 51→52 and computer 8→9. The new parent cells expose variety rows that have no source of their own.
- **Intra-row duplicates:** 18 new duplicates were locked as genuine.
