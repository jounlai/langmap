# Review 787 — ancient toasts (owner 2026-11-18: 「乾杯の古代語が少ないのはちょっと残念。きっと、たくさんあるはずなのに」)

Closed 2026-11-18. Four draft-only agents searched 117 historical rows for a toast that a text actually records. A further check of FineWeb-2 web text covered Turkic and Iranian toasts. 26 cheers cells were added: 23 historical and 3 modern. Cheers now has 273 cells.

## Historical toasts (source in each cell's note)
- **Greek:** el_grc προπίνω σοι (Xenophon, Anabasis 7.3.26). el_kath εἰς ὑγείαν.
- **Egyptian:** egy n kꜣ=ṯ "for your ka" (banquet scene in the tomb of Paheri). The hieroglyph spelling is ours.
- **Chinese and its readings:**
  - och 萬壽無疆 (Shijing, Qiyue), with Baxter-Sagart readings; zh_wenyan_edu has the same phrase.
  - zh_tang 上壽 (Jiu Tangshu) and vi_han 上壽 (Shiji).
  - ja_kanbun 壽を為す (Shiji, the Hongmen banquet).
  - zh_yuan 滿飲一杯 (單刀會). The readings of 滿 and 杯 were rebuilt from their rhyme classes.
- **Japanese:** ojp 阿佐受袁勢 佐佐 (Kojiki, song 40).
- **Germanic:**
  - non heill ver þú (Lokasenna 53, Skírnismál 37).
  - ang wes hāl. Old English used it as a greeting; it became the drinking salute later.
  - got hails (Anthologia Latina 285). The text may be Vandalic.
  - gmh trinc daz ûz (Helmbrecht).
  - de_lut es gilt (DWB s.v. gelten).
  - en_em I drink to you (Shakespeare).
- **Spanish:** es_sgl brindez (Covarrubias 1611).
- **Persian and Turkish:** fa_clas نوش بادت (Nezami, Anvari). ota عشقكزه (Redhouse 1890).
- **Hebrew and Aramaic:**
  - he_mis חמרא לחייכון (Tosefta Shabbat 7:8).
  - arc חַמְרָא וְחַיֵּי לְפוּם רַבָּנַן (Bavli Shabbat 67b).
- **Arabic:** ar_qur هنيئا. The Quran has no toast, so this is a stand-in; the note says so.
- **Old Tagalog:** h_tagalog tagay (Noceda & Sanlúcar 1754), meaning the round of drink.

## Modern toasts confirmed from web text (FineWeb-2)
- ky ден соолук үчүн

All three were seen in raised-glass contexts.

## Rejected
- **Phrases that never appeared as toasts:** uz, tk, ba, cv and ug health phrases. They only appeared in "good for your health" prose.
- **kmr noşî can:** it means "bon appétit".
- **zh_han 上壽:** held. The row's Later Han reading could not be sourced (StarLing is robots-barred).
- **No recorded drinking formula:** fro, oar, hbo, egy_dem, sa, hy_grab, goh, cu, pal and about 60 other rows. Those cells stay empty.

## Guards
- ang wes hāl and got hails are now also the row's hello. This is a genuine double use, so both were locked.
