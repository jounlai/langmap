## Notable fills
- Hiri Motu, Motu, Pohnpeian, Arrernte, Noongar, Woleaian and Rotokas now have husband and wife. Several of them use one word for both, so those cells take the spouse route.
- Bislama gained 28 everyday words, Pijin 31 and Yapese 31.
- Harari gained five, four, hundred, horse, salt and black.
- Kanite gained yesterday, the same word ega as its tomorrow. That matches the row's existing tomorrow route.
