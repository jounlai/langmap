# Review 790 — Bible sweeps B2/B3 and a Wiktionary gloss sweep

Closed 2026-11-18. This was the second half of the owner's one-hour push. Five agents mined about 115 New Testament texts already on disk, using verse pairs checked against the English WEB text. A sixth ran a gloss sweep over the Wiktionary dump. 345 cells were applied.

## Notable fills
- **Kosraean:** king tokosra, east kutulap, island tuka, earthquake kusrusr and twenty longoul.
- **Rossel Island (Yele):** money ndapî, the island's famous shell money, which occurs 224 times in its NT.
- **Bororo:** right pemegareu jagwai "good side" and left pegareu jagwai "bad side".
- **Choctaw:** right ibbak isht impaka, "the hand one eats with".
- **Lingala:** left is "woman's hand" and right is "man's hand".
- **Huave:** language ombeay is the same word as mouth.
- **Tear built from eye + water:** Huli de iba, Hote mathôk, Mam taʼl twitz, Nahuatl ixayotl and Mussau rarum mata.
- **Arabic varieties (Wiktionary):** Egyptian and Moroccan Arabic gained basic words such as snake, left, window, thousand and mirror. Most other Arabic variety rows still lack left, right, snake and wife.
- **Plains Cree:** island, snake and money, matching the Cree row.

## Skipped or removed at apply
- **tmh, rif:** the Tifinagh spellings were converted by the agent, so they were not used.
- **ko_jeju east 세.**
- **huv yellow:** the bitter route for a Spanish loan.
- **mxv right:** it is spelt the same as red, and tones are unmarked.
- **bzd lion:** possibly the jaguar word.
- **Drafts with empty IPA.**
- **toc lion león:** the accent read as tone; it was dropped after the tone guard.
- **agt earthquake IPA:** g changed to ɡ.
