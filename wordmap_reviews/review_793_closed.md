# Review 793 — leftover holds resolved, flagged cells corrected, two route fixes

Closed 2026-11-19. The owner asked for this, in order (「順番でやって」).

## 1. Route fixes
- **kpv king кан:** plain → khan. The Turkic khan word, by resemblance to its neighbours koi кан, mns хо̄н and xal хан.
- **monkey enkima/nkima in nyn, cgg and lg:** plain → nkima. These are reflexes of Proto-Bantu *nkɪ́mà, like ttj, kg, ki and jmc.
