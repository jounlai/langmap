## Notable fills
- **One word for husband and wife:** Wallisian ʻohoana and Ngäbere muko.
- **Left and right by gender:** Makhuwa left is "the woman's side" and right "the man's side".
- **Week = Sunday:** Pangasinan week is simba, the same word as the row's Sunday.
- **Tear built on "water":** Edo ameve, Ga yaafonu and Dangme vonyu.
- **Baoulé twenty:** ablaɔn, "ten + two" fused into one word.
