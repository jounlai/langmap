# Review 800 — dragon round 2 (448 rows); Bible tails for Africa, Turkmen, Abkhaz

Closed 2026-11-19.

## Dragon, round 2: 390 → 448 rows
- **Sources:**
  - Wiktionary entries glossed "dragon": khw, szl, cng, cop ⲇⲣⲁⲕⲱⲛ, rom, bxr луу, ii ꇐ, se guovdi, xkz druk, sa उरङ्ग, ksw, yai aždahor and slr boğunaq. slr boğunaq is also thunder.
  - Revelation 12/20 in NWT texts on wol.jw.org: cv, tsz, su, ada, gaa, ts, lua, kg, tll, ay, tvl, gil, war, quz, nso, loz, ssw, nbl, mfe, crs, gcf and others. The big-snake renderings take the serpent route: tum chinjoka, ttj ekijoka, apw tłʼiishcho, yua nojoch kaan and ak ɔwɔ kɛse.
  - King James 1611 for en_em.
  - Ossetian залиаг калм, built on калм "snake".
- **Arabic parity copies of تنين:** applied only where the row has no clash. ar_sy, ar_lb, ar_jo, ar_ps, ar_lev and ar_ma were dropped. In the Levantine rows تنين is the word for "two" (tnēn), and in Moroccan the reading differs.
- **Not applied:**
  - Forms found only in the English translation table, with no entry of their own: av, dar, lbe, lez, ddo, nog, ug, ce, oc, vec and rm.
  - IPA joined from the parts' entries: nv and gd.
  - Accented Spanish loans in toned rows: tzh and mxv.

## Gap cells (Bible tails)
- **Sidaama:** husband minaanna, built on "house" + "father", and wife minaama, built on "house" + "mother".
- **Dinka:** snake këpiiny, "thing of the ground", and language thok, which is also mouth.
- **Rutoro:** thunder enkuba (Mark 3:17).
- **Cindau:** husband, thousand, island, thunder, window, kiss, gold and earthquake.
- **Makonde:** camel, Greece, kiss and right. Right is kunkono, built on the hand word.
- **Turkmen:** spider möý (Job 8:14, Isa 59:5).
- **Abkhaz:** cotton абамба (Esther 1:6) and elephant аслан.
- **Tarifit:** spider ⵇⵓⵏⴷⵄⴰ.
- **Skipped:** mer king mũnene, because Matthew is the only source and its neighbours use mũthamaki.
