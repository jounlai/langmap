## Dragon, historical rows: 11 cells
- **ko_mid 미르:** from the Hunmong jahoe (1527), "龍 미르 료ᇰ". 미르 is the attested native word.
- **ja_heian 龍 /tatu/:** from the Wamyō Ruijushō (938), which gives 太都.
- **zh_tang 龍 luawŋ:** Pulleyblank's Early Middle Chinese, via Wiktionary. This replaces the round-1 ytenx draft, which was dropped because ytenx is robots-barred.
- **Other historical rows:**
  - juc muduri (Grube 1896).
  - uga tnn, "dragon, sea monster".
  - ave aži, the serpent of Aži Dahāka.
  - hit Illuyanka, the same word as the row's snake.
  - it_dan drago, from Purgatorio XXXII 131.
- **Proto rows, using published reconstructions glossed "dragon":**
  - p_ine *h₁ógʷʰis, "snake; the mythic serpent".
  - p_tun *muduri.
  - p_sit *m-bru(ŋ/k).
- **Not applied:** ptrk *sārᶻgan, where "dragon" is only the second sense.
