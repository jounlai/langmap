- **Vietnamese regional rows (34 cells):**
  - vi_c and vi_s gained snake, monkey, paper, yellow, east, window, week, Tuesday and Wednesday.
  - Southern regional words: bắp (maize), kiếng (mirror), xà bông (soap) and ngàn (thousand). Central/Southern kiss is hun.
  - IPA from the project's dialect generator (~/langmap-work/vidial). It agrees 100% with the rows' tone classes, and r is ɹ per the owner ruling.
- **Mainland SE Asia, other rows:**

- **South Asia (50 cells):**
  - Dogri (Hindi–Dogri dictionary): snake सप्प, monkey बांदर, wife and husband घरैआली/घरैआला ("of the house"), twenty and thousand.
  - Bhojpuri (Bhojpuri–Hindi Shabdkosh, archive.org): snake, gold, wife मेहरारू, money, mirror ऐना and yesterday कल्ह.
  - Konkani (Sardesai's dictionary, checked against the Romi NWT): king, kiss उमो, camel, earthquake and tear.
- **Dragon, Thai:** the th note now records what a Thai reader explained, confirmed in Wiktionary. มังกร is used for both 龍 and the Western dragon. Thai keeps นาค for the Buddhist nāga, and names the zodiac Year of the Dragon มะโรง, from Old Khmer roṅ.
- **Notation fixes:** fj kiss and mkz yellow changed g to ɡ.
