# Review 804 — Americas: wol.jw.org Bibles for 20 indigenous rows (dragon now 480 rows)

Closed 2026-11-19. 95 cells were applied.

## Sources
- **wol.jw.org full Bibles:** yua, tzo, tzh, toj, mxv, tsz, ay, gn, qu, quz, quy, arn and guc. The Navajo Bible is the 1985 one, publication sbi1.
- **NT only:** kek, ctu and ngu.
- **Partial Bibles:** quc, mam and gym.

## Notable fills
- **Dragon:**
  - Navajo naʼashǫ́ʼiitsoh, "big lizard", route other. Wiktionary glosses it "dragon".
  - Wayuu wüi miyoʼu, "big snake", built on the row's snake word, route serpent.
  - Tzeltal and Chol dragón. Their Bibles keep the row's own snake word separate in Rev 12:9.
- **Basic words:**
  - Tojolabal husband and wife, tatamal and cheʼumal.
  - Bolivian and Ayacucho Quechua wife warmi.
  - Wayuu thousand miit and window wentaana.
  - Guerrero Nahuatl left and right, opochma and yekma.
- **Mixtec:** butter, cheese, India, island, bat, mirror, paper, silk, spider and thunder. The Bible's tone marks were dropped to match this toneless row.

## Not applied
- **nch:** the library is "náhuatl de la Huasteca", but its spelling (kouatl) differs from the row's (koatl), so the variety is uncertain.
- **Agent holds:**
  - Quechua and Guarani dragon phrases built on a Spanish snake loan.
  - Aymara, Bolivian Quechua and Mapudungun native words where the Bible's orthography differs from the row's.

## Guards and fixes
- **Duplicates locked as genuine:**
  - book = paper in yua juʼun, quc wuj, ctu juñ, toj juʼun and mxv tutu.
  - gym dobo, both earth and earthquake.
- **quz king:** stress removed from the monosyllable rey.
