# Review 805 — Caucasus/Iran, Southern Africa, PNG and Australia, European minority rows; dragon variety rows (dragon now 509 rows)

Closed 2026-11-19. 132 cells were applied.

## Caucasus and Iran (wol.jw.org, ebible, Wiktionary)
- **Western Armenian:** snake, king, lion, gold, tear, east, language, camel, monkey, paper and spider, from the Western Armenian Bible (sbi1) with Wiktionary IPA.
- **Mingrelian (NWT):** dragon დრაკონი, earthquake, east, gold, left, right, mirror, money, tear and window.
- **Dragon in Sorani and Uyghur:** both are azhdaha forms.
- **Dari:** Friday جمعه and uncle کاکا (split).

## Southern Africa (wol.jw.org NWT)
- **Dragon in seven rows:**
  - st drakone.
  - tn noga e kgolo, "big snake".
  - ve ḽigweṋa and bem icingʼwena. Both put the augmentative prefix on the crocodile word, so they take the other route.
  - nd umgobho, kj ongadu and ng oshikoko take the plain route.
- **Kiss:** filled in eight Bantu rows from Gen 29:11 and 27:26.
- **Sepedi:** bread senkgwa, left letshadi and right letona, plus cheese, butter, tear and uncle rangwane (split).

## New Guinea and Australia (Bibles on disk)
- **Dragon:**
  - Huli abagua, the Leviathan word of Job 41.
  - Arrernte apmwe kwatye-arenye, "water-dwelling snake".
  - Enga kanopato pambea, Kanite osifaʼve (the snake word) and others.
- **Other words:**
  - Warlpiri right jungarni, "straight".
  - Enga kiss nunu, found in all 15 kiss verses.
- **Not applied:** Motu dragon huala, which is the crocodile word.

## Europe
- **IPA-bearing cells:**
  - Öömrang twenty and language (printed IPA).
  - Mirandese thousand.
  - Yiddish cotton, earthquake, octopus and wisdom tooth (YIVO spelling).
- **Dragon:** gv dragan, oc dragon and rm dragun.
- **Not applied:** about 97 sourced words that have no printed IPA, in Manx, Gaelic, Faroese, Cornish, Breton and others.

## Dragon in variety rows
- **Own sources:**
  - gsw Traach (Alemannic).
  - ko_hg 룡. ko_yb was corrected from 용 to 룡, since Wiktionary tags 룡 as the North Korean and Yanbian form.
- **Parity copies:** th_n, th_s, vmf, ksh and ext.
- **Not applied:** the Levantine and Moroccan copies, which would clash with the rows' word for two or for Monday.
