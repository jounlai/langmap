# Review 806 — new row: Pela (bxd)

Closed 2026-11-19. The owner suggested it: 「Pela, a Burmish language spoken in 德宏Dehong Prefecture of 雲南Yunnan Province」.

## Row
- **Code:** bxd (Glottolog pela1242).
- **Family:** Sino-Tibetan (Lolo-Burmese, Burmish, Maruic).
- **Speakers:** about 500 (2005). Source: Dai et al. 2007, via en.wikipedia.
- **Pin:** the Glottolog point near Longchuan, Dehong. Most villages are in Mangshi, but the Zaiwa (atb) pin already sits on Mangshi at the same coordinates.
- **Names and description:** names in 19 UI languages. zh 波拉语 and sw Kipela are Wikipedia titles; the rest are transliterations. The description is in 19 languages, built from en.wikipedia and the source data.

## Cells: 76
- **Sources:**
  - Lexibank sagartst, doculect Bola (Sagart et al. 2019, from Huang Bufan 1992).
  - Wiktionary's 179 Pela entries, used for cross-checks and for love, bear, book, thanks, tea, gold, money, thousand, China, tobacco and chili.
- **Notation:** follows the Achang row (also from sagartst). The surface keeps the tone digits, and the IPA uses Chao letters. Tense vowels are written with creaky ̰.
- **Route choices:**
  - thunder sky, as in acn.
  - tea leaf.
  - wife woman (mi³¹).
  - money silver (ŋø⁵⁵ "silver").
  - thousand qian, from 千.
  - China other, from 中国.
  - tobacco medicine.
  - chili hot ("hot ginger").
  - twenty tens.
- **Homophones locked as genuine duplicates:**
  - ta³¹ is "one", "eat" and "child". Wiktionary has these as three separate entries.
  - ŋø⁵⁵ is "silver/money" and "love".
  - sak⁵⁵ is "tree" and "new".

## Gaps recorded
- **cat, hello and night:** each has "—" with unattestedReason unsourced, and an entry in validate_wordmap_data.js MODERN_UNSOURCED_ALLOW. Night and person exist only in Mann 1998, whose tone system differs from the row's.
- **we:** one form only, "we (incl.)" in Sagart, so the route is unknown. The route ratchet went from 36 to 37.
- **foot:** kʰji⁵³. The source has no word for the whole leg, so the route is unknown. The ratchet went from 22 to 23.
