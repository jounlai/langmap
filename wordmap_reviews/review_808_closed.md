## Dragon
- **Sakha (Yakut) луо:** a reader, 北村 聡志, pointed this out. It is confirmed in the Yakut explanatory dictionary on sakhatyla.ru, which has no robots.txt. That dictionary compares it with Mongolian and Uyghur луу, Old Turkic lü and Chinese 龍, so the route is long. Sakha Wikipedia uses it for 龍 and for a three-headed dragon, луо-кыыл. The note mentions the Russian loan дракон.
