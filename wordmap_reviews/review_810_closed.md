# Review 810 — weekdays from wol.jw.org daily-text headings (235 cells, 53 rows)

Closed 2026-11-19.

## Source
On wol.jw.org, each language library's daily-text pages (Examining the Scriptures Daily) carry the weekday name in the day's heading. Seven consecutive days (15–22 Nov) give a full set, even for libraries that have no Bible. 207 row/library pairs were fetched, about 2.5 s apart. About 70 libraries head their daily texts in their own language.

## Fills
- **Rows that had no weekdays and now have all six:**
  - dyu: Tɛnɛlon … Dimansilon.
  - kri: Mɔnde … Sɔnde.
  - gcf: Lendi … Dimanch.
  - nzi, ada, ngu, cak and bbc.
- **Count weeks in East and Southern Africa:** ki, kam, bem, nyn, ttj, lue, ng, kj, ndc and tll. Sundays named after the week (Kiumia, Wakyumwa, Pa Mulungu) take the other route, following kqn.
- **Pacific:**
  - tvl: Aso Lua … Aso Sa.
  - gil: Kauabong … Taabati, the native ordinals. The row's existing Monday is the loan Mante.
  - pon: Niare … Rahn Sarawi.
  - wls: Feliatolu … ʻAhotapu.
- **Americas:** Mayan, Zapotec, Mixtec, Quechua and Purépecha rows. The Spanish-loan days follow rows that already use loans.
- **Others:**
  - alt: Russian loans, confirmed in Wiktionary.
  - ca_va Friday, hyw, pnb, nia, iba, ksw, ig, tiv, iso, rn, yao, gaa, seh, ngl, aja, kmb, mos, bci, bum and sid.
- **Routes:**
  - Spanish and French domingo/dimanche take lord.
  - English Saturday takes saturn.
  - Akan- and Gbe-type day names take plain, with Monday other, as in the ak and ee rows.

## Not applied (held by the agent)

- **Headings not in the row's language or spelling:** gn, ay, quc, vmw and fon. guc adds a suffix, and srm marks tones.
- **Variety check under 5 matches:** fan, ctu, ahk and mixtec.
