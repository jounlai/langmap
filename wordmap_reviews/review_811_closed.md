## Japanese and Korean dialects
Sources: Lee & Hasegawa 2011 (lexibank leejaponic; each list was matched to its row on 5 or more cells), JLect (robots bars only login/admin), and Wiktionary dialect tags.
- **Kagoshima:** spider こっ koʔ, snake へっ heʔ, wife うっかた, mirror かがん, tear, paper and tobacco たぼこ.
- **Kansai:** goosebumps さぶいぼ, "cold wart", for Osaka and Kyoto.
- **Okinawan:** earthquake ねー (from old なゐ), jellyfish くらじ and monkey.
- **Ishigaki:** husband budu, wife tudzɨ, snake habu, left, right, spider and thunder.
- **Sendai:** wife がが, which is also mother. Locked as a genuine duplicate.
- **Other rows:**
  - Tsugaru money じぇんこ.
  - Hiroshima thunder どんどろ.
  - Miyako black.
  - Busan dragonfly, tomorrow and older brother.
  - Yanbian mobile phone 써우지, from Mandarin 手机.

## Ancient rows
- **Heian Japanese weekdays 日曜 … 土曜:** the court calendars (具注暦) and the Midō Kanpakuki record each day's luminary.
- **Edo Japanese:** right, paper, yellow, India 天竺, lion, cotton 木綿, camel, twenty, uncle, dragon 竜 and tobacco. Tobacco is attested in the Keichō Kenmonshū.
- **Stand-ins for computer, each with a note:**
  - Syriac ܟܪܡܐ, "counting table, abacus".
  - Middle Persian and Parthian "accountant".
- **Other ancient cells:**
  - Ancient Greek week ἑβδομάς (Hippocratic).
  - Sumerian camel amsi-ḫarran, "caravan elephant".
- **Not applied:**
  - egy: hieroglyph strings assembled from sign codes.
  - kaw: Javanese script transliterated by the agent.
