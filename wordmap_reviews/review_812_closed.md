# Review 812 — Libyan and Sudanese Arabic from print vocabularies, Sinitic weekdays, Mesoamerica, Early Modern Korean

Closed 2026-11-19. 190 cells were applied.

## Arabic dialects (read from page images)
- **Sudanese, Hillelson 1925, *Sudan Arabic: English–Arabic Vocabulary* (public domain): 37 cells.**
  - The basic words: camel, cheese, cotton, east, island, king, language, left, right, mirror, paper, soap, spider, tear, wife, husband, butter, twenty and thousand.
  - Snake dābi.
  - The week is جمعة, which also means Friday. The weekdays are written as يوم + day, as Hillelson prints them.
- **Libyan, Dickinson 2004, *Spoken Libyan Arabic* (Tripoli): 10 cells.**
  - Snake, money, camel, Tuesday and gold.
  - Wednesday arbħa, which shows the Libyan change of ʕ to ħ.

## Sinitic (dial-syn plus MCPDict town readings)
- Saturday was empty in about 45 rows that already had Monday to Friday. Each row now has its own weekday prefix with 六, read from its town table.
- Sunday where the dial-syn table lists the town. Some towns say 礼拜天 even though their row uses 星期 for the other days.
- Tear, money, right, language and island where the table has the town.
- **Dropped at apply:** yue_zs and hsn weekdays. These were built from the row's own Sunday prefix, with no dial-syn entry for the town.
- **Lexical-import debt raised 58 → 67:** the new 礼拜X/星期X cells leave the older 星期X cells in yue and yue_ts standing beside siblings that differ. Every one of these cells is sourced.

## Mesoamerica
- **Mezquital Otomi (WOLD, Lastra), 17 cells:** left, right, yellow, money, paper, language, east, and the native weekdays yopa "two-day" and hñupa "three-day".
- **Tlaxiaco Mixtec (Auderset et al. 2023), 9 cells:** snake koo, yellow, maize, money and twenty oko (vigesimal).
- **Rainbow, from Revelation 4:3 and 10:1:** Ngäbere, Bribri and Huave.
- **Kuna earthquake:** negabandur.

## Early Modern Korean (Underwood 1890 and Gale 1897, page images), 21 cells
- **Weekdays:** Gale 1897 already lists 월요일 … 일요일 beside the missionary 례ᄇᆡ일.
- **Other words:**
  - Rice 쌀 and thousand 천.
  - Grandmother 할머니, route split. 외조모 is the mother's mother.
  - Camel 약ᄃᆡ and dragon 룡.
  - Milky Way 은하슈.
  - Cockcrow ᄭᅩᆨᄭᅬ오, "the crowing of a cock".
- **ko_mid bread:** ·ᄯᅥᆨ, the steamed rice cake, entered as a stand-in with a note (Worin seokbo 1459).
