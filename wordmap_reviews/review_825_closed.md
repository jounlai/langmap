# Review 825 — North Korean row (ko_kp): cells copied from South Korean (closed 2026-11-20)

Owner asked: does North Korea also say 버터? Then: check every North Korean cell that matches the South Korean one.

## Scope
- ko_kp had 139 cells identical to ko; 15 already differed.
- Each of the 139 was checked against a North Korean-standard dictionary: the Chongryon 학우서방 digital 조선말사전 on archive.org, 41k entries, which marks rejected forms with × and gives the South form.
- A second source came from Wiktionary (kaikki dump) and the Wikipedia page on North–South differences.

## Changed (7 words, 2 IPA fixes)
- wife 아내 → 안해 anhɛ: the dictionary entry; Wiktionary "North Korea, Yanbian".
- butter 버터 → 빠다 p͈ada: the dictionary example "빠다를 바른 빵"; Wiktionary "common in North Korea".
- maize 옥수수 → 강냉이 kaŋnɛŋi: the dictionary has "옥수수 ×☞ 강냉이"; Wiktionary tags it North Korea. The route grain → place, since Wiktionary gives "likely from 강남 江南 Jiangnan".
- cotton 면화 → 목화 mokʰwa: the dictionary has "면화 ×☞ 목화". The route stays other ("tree flower").
- camel 낙타 → 락타 ɾaktʰa: initial ㄹ kept, as in the row's 룡 and 래일; Wiktionary "North Korea standard spelling".
- green 초록 → 록색 ɾoks͈ɛk̚: the dictionary says the basic colour name is 록색. 풀색 is a near-synonym.
- tooth 이 → 이발 ip͈al: the dictionary's full entry, pronounced [이빨]. 이 is mostly a word-forming element there.
- IPA fixes:
  - fish: mulɡoɡi → mulk͈oɡi (dictionary [물꼬기], as the ko row).
  - hiccup: t͈alk͈ukt͈ɕil → t͈alk͈uk̚t͈ɕil (as the ko row).
