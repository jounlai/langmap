# Review 826 — Yanbian Korean row (ko_yb): cells identical to Seoul (closed 2026-11-20)

Owner asked to check Hamgyong and Yanbian too, and suggested Yanbian might say 황유 or 빠다 for butter.

## Method
- All 135 ko_yb cells that matched ko were checked against the following sources.
- Press: Chinese Korean press (연변일보, 길림신문, 인민넷 조선어판), with a 323-article corpus.
- Wiktionary: the ko.wiktionary category 연변말.
- Survey: 오선화·한성우 2023 (288 speakers).
- Dictionary: the 조선말사전 database.

## Changed
- camel 낙타 → 락타 ɾaktʰa.
- wife 아내 → 안해 anhɛ.
- green 초록 → 록색 ɾoks͈ɛk̚: corpus counts 록색 18, 녹색 0, 초록 1.
- fish IPA mulɡoɡi → mulk͈oɡi.

## Kept, against the North Korean row
These keep the Seoul word because the Chinese Korean press uses it:
- butter 버터: no 황유 and no 빠다 found in the press. 길림신문 writes "치즈, 버터, 요구르트".
- maize 옥수수: 14 hits, 강냉이 0.
- tooth 이.
- germany 독일; india 인도.
- cotton 목화: 길림신문, 인민넷.

Colloquial Chinese loans (맨보 面包, 로퍼 老婆) exist in speech but are not in print, so none was used.
