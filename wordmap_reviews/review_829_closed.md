# Word Map review 829: every cell's etymology against Wiktionary

2026-11-25. Every spelling on a routed word (about 75,000) was looked up in English Wiktionary; 20,710 cells have an etymology there for the same language. The etymology's source words were compared with those of the other cells in the same word. 690 cells pointed more to another route than to their own; those whose entry does not mean the word were set aside, and the remaining 629 were read (376 in a route, 253 plain).

- **In a route**: nearly all of the 376 only share a remoter ancestor with another route (Celtic and Germanic from one Indo-European root, a loan and its donor, a semantic route). Corrected: Tatar and Bashkir әфлисун 'orange' (from Russian апельсин, so named for China, not Portugal; the Bashkir word is the same loan as the Tatar) and Tuvan быштак 'cheese' (Turkic *bïšlaq).
- **Plain**: cells whose entry states the etymon without a hedge were moved into the existing route: 175 cells in 55 words.
- **Left alone**: hedged entries (perhaps, probably, either … or); sources that disagree (person bn, as, sd: manuṣya or mānuṣa; cuckoo ml: kokila or DEDR kuyil; tongue my; one nmf); nose pzh and ssf (*mujiŋ, not the route's *ijuŋ); rice mg (a blend); cuckoo es, gl, pt, fur, ro; words with semantic routes (uncle, foot, twenty, wife).
- **Browser, Chromium, Firefox and WebKit**: Langle then Escape, language change ends the journey, right-to-left step counter, Sinosphere tag fallback and 320px width all pass.
