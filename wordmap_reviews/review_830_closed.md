# Word Map review 830: meanings against Wiktionary, and dialect rows against their parent language

2026-11-25.

- **Meaning**: 28,338 cells have a Wiktionary entry for the same language. 1,116 entries do not name the word's meaning in the part kept; 366 were left after synonyms and cross-reference entries were set aside, and the list of them was read, with the cell notes for about 45. Nearly all are homographs or one word with two senses (month and moon, air and wind). Corrected: Old Church Slavonic cat котъка, attested only as 'anchor' (Vasmer, citing the Codex Suprasliensis; Wiktionary lists no Old Church Slavonic word under Proto-Slavic *kotъ), is now blank. Earlier reviews (20 and others) had raised it and left it open.
- **Dialects**: 102 sets of rows of one language share a spelling but not a route. Where the pronunciation shows the same word, the row takes the parent's route: 34 cells (wind in nine Englishes, gold and island in three and two Portuguese rows, Arabic nose and you, and others). Left alone: rows read differently (Japanese, Ryukyuan and kanbun readings), the Proto-Japonic-Koreanic row (hypothetical, so without routes), twenty in Belgian, Swiss and Luxembourg French, and semantic routes still marked as needing a source.
