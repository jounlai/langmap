# Review 833: Teochew, Shantou and Chaozhou

2026-11-25. The owner asked for separate rows if the two city dialects differ much, otherwise a note.

- **Measured** on the MCPDict tables 潮州 and 汕頭 (3,482 shared characters, notation made alike): 77% have the same readings. The eight tone categories are the same (5 readings differ in category); tone values are close (Chaozhou 332 and 25, Shantou 33 and 35; the 7th tone 21 and 31). Nearly all other differences are regular finals: ieŋ/iaŋ, ueŋ/uaŋ, iou/iau, ie/io, iẽ/iõ, and ə/ɯ in the notation. Of the Han Map's characters 107 of 126 agree; of the Word Map's syllables that the tables cover, 120 of 137.
- **Decision**: one row, with a note in the descriptions (19 languages, Word Map and Han Map) that the readings are Shantou's. The Han Map region no longer says Chaozhou city.
- **Aligned with Shantou**: 萬 buaŋ, 男 nam, 朝 tsiau, tomato 番茄 kio (the cell had the Chaozhou final and Peng'im g).
