**Not added:** Southern Uzbek (uzs). No Afghan-dialect word list was found; what could be filled would be standard Uzbek respelled.

**Dropped in review:**
- Ibanag: 15 words known only from an 1892 grammar, plus head and hundred (sources differ on the final glottal).
- Kinaray-a: blood, tongue, mouth (final glottal not marked) and hello.
- Aklanon: wheel (a cart) and bread (one kind of loaf).
- Jarai: egg (from a phrase) and thanks (only the verb). Rade: daughter ("son or daughter").
- Chadian Arabic: right and bread.
- Sangir: book, money, wine, cat, fart, blue, thunder, uncle, daughter, hundred (final glottal inferred or unconfirmed), wife (sources differ).
- Vitality values that were copied from a sister row or estimated.

**Held:**
- Gawri is tonal; the Gawri-script sources write no tone, so neither does the row (Baart 1997 not reached).
- Hawrami is in MacKenzie's transcription, Luhon dialect; fire, i, you and drink are blank because only other dialects were found.
- Tai Nüa: 48 of 126 spellings follow the Tai Le letter values from the IDS transcription (131 attested spellings agree with that rule).
- Karo Batak e: ə unless a source writes é.
- Names and descriptions in the 19 UI languages were drafted for this round; about half of the names could be compared with Wikidata labels.
