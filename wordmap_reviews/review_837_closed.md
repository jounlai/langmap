# Review 837 — cells left in `plain` that belong to a route

2026-11-26. The new rows of review 836 showed many older cells still in `plain` beside a routed sister with the same word. Every cell was compared with the expert cognate codings of about 60 lexibank datasets (ABVD, Grollemund's Bantu, IE-CoR, UraLex, peirosst, sagartst, zhangst, savelyevturkic, dravlex, utoaztecan and others). 1,501 cells in plain matched an entry whose cognate set holds routed cells of the same family; eight agents judged them family by family against the route labels, and proposed routes for cognate sets with four or more plain cells.

## Applied
- **1691 cells** left `plain`: 1209 to existing routes, 482 to 66 new routes.
- By family of the row: Austronesian 482, Atlantic-Congo 409, Sino-Tibetan 221, Austroasiatic 96, Uto-Aztecan 91, Uralic 76, Niger-Congo 59, Indo-European 38, Dravidian 32, Mongolic 28.
- **Back to plain (3):** Ganda rain enkuba (not *mbúdà; Grollemund has it in another set), Kavalan you aisu (ACD *iSu), Maranao nose ngirong (ACD *ŋijuŋ).

The evidence for most cells is the expert cognate set plus a regular-looking form; a second source (ACD, BLR3, STEDT, Wiktionary) was read for part of them and for every new route's proto-form.

## Dropped
- Rejected by the agents: about 360 of 1,501 (the set is wider than the label, the matched entry is another word, sources disagree, one source only in a deep family).
- Dropped after that: the calls the agents called doubtful (about 100), cells that rested on a neighbouring language's form, loans without a source (rice, silk in Karelian, Olonets, Veps, Votic, Rusyn rice), three irregular forms (Hote eye, Nauruan five, Gorontalo left).
- Routes not made: twenty *ma-puSaN (that word's routes are structural), snake *nipay, mouth *qawa, bone *duRi (clash with *suRi), green *ma-rawa, one *ókó, Uto-Aztecan sun and second-person *ʼïm, Mon-Khmer tooth *srang (same root as the route ksang), Tibeto-Burman louse *syar, Karen sun, Tibetan red (identical spelling only).

## Held
- left: Atayal and Seediq are in *wiRi, ABVD has them in another set. hand: Garo and rGyalrong are in *lak, STEDT has *d-yak.
- you: Bikol and Kapampangan ika (ACD puts ka under *ka). mouth kha, sleep tidur, tooth gigi: the labels could be widened.
- Possible further Bantu routes: fish *cómbá, night *tʊ́kʊ̀ cells.
