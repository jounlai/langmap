## Left out
- Taken out at the checks: Saterland Frisian Wíend and Ottoman یل (fart, the same word as wind in the row), Northern Sámi (octopus, jellyfish) and Taiwanese Hakka (jellyfish), because their dialect rows (Lule and Skolt Sámi, Hailu) have no cell yet.
- fart: word lists that give only the verb (most of IDS, the Turkic and Caucasian lists); Kosraean eng (the word for wind); Zhuang, Naxi, Aromanian and Tujia (spelling or IPA would be composed); three Sinitic rows whose MCPDict point is not the row's place.
- octopus: Itbayat koyta ("big octopus"), Manx, Tibetan and Jinan (IPA or reading composed).
- jellyfish: North Korean (only the standard word), Tunisian حريقة (stinging kinds only), Skolt Sámi (IPA composed), Tetum (an unreferenced entry), Inuktitut (another dialect's word).
- ladybird: Scottish Gaelic (IPA composed). For jellyfish and ladybird most small languages have no dictionary entry online; those cells stay empty.
