# Review 847 — the 19-language descriptions and names of the sixteen new rows

2026-11-26. Review 836 left the descriptions and names of its 16 rows read only in en and ja. Three agents compared the other 17 UI languages with the English: 49 description fixes in 15 rows, 6 names aligned with that language's Wikipedia title. No factual error was found in ko, zh, yue, vi, th; the serious ones elsewhere were a wrong island (pt Flores: Azores "das Flores" → "de Flores"), Finnic read as Finnish (de, sw, ru, uk), a palatalised-sounding "soft [ð]" (ru, uk), Korean family levels inverted (ibg), ungrammatical Hebrew numerals and a non-word, and dropped clauses (ar shu).
