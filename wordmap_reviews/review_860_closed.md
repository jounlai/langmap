- **child, route `koan`** (Proto-Austroasiatic *koan): the Min 囝 cells rest on a contested substrate-loan claim, and without them only 3 cells are left. 5 cells become plain.
- **cloud, Armenian ամպ ×3 left out of *nébʰos:** the Armenian page says "formal difficulties". The label example was removed with it.
- **cloud, route `exulen`** (Proto-Mongolic *exülen): it reaches 4 cells only with a Classical Mongolian form that has no page of its own.
- **smoke, route `kwinhv`** (Proto-North Caucasian): the macro-family is not generally accepted. Proto-Lezgic *ƛuma stays.
