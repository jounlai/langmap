# Review 863 — six basic verbs and adjectives: walk, give, say, know, big, small

2026-11-28. Follows review 862.

## Process
- **Drafting:** 15 family drafters.
- **Merge:** 3,027 cells drafted. 79 of them were dropped at merge under the strict rules (see below).
- **Verification:** 13 verifiers.
  - Three verifiers first returned verdicts based on the drafters' quoted evidence (lists 4, 7 and 12). They were sent back and re-read every cell at its source. The other ten were told the same before they finished.
  - Totals: 2,913 ok, 36 fixed, 78 dropped. Another 24 were skipped by me (below).
- **Citation forms:** verbs follow each row's habit for eat/drink/come; adjectives follow good/full. Korean uses the dictionary -다 form, Japanese the base form.

## My own decisions
- **walk vs go:** a source that lists 'go' first, or only, is dropped.
  - Germanic: sv, no, nn, goh, non.
  - Slavic: ru, uk, cs, sk, dsb, mk.
  - Indo-Aryan: hi, ur, gu, pnb.
  - Turkic: ky, nog, ug, alt, ba, crh.
  - Others: udm, xal, bxr, ess, itl, mn (a phrase), tzm, qu, quz, mtq, zu, xh, ace, bzh, fj, cnh, xct, xct_litpr, el_grc, ja_heian (ありく is not necessarily on foot).
- **Two sources, two different words:** dropped.
  - know: ar (علم/عرف), be, hy, mhr, sma, kn, nan (Taipei dial-syn lists 知影).
  - say: sl, ium (tone), kmc (tone), ko_jeju (ᄀᆞᆮ다 vs mal he-), wbp.
  - big: bxr, evn, zh_cq (three MCPDict readings), blt (IPA).
  - small: ce, koi, ko_jl.
  - walk: sel, mnc, izh, ce, kl.
- **Sinitic compounds:** dialect cells for 晓得/知道/走路 drop when their readings were built from citation tones. That is 17 rows; zh and zh_tw 知道 are kept from Wiktionary.
- **Japanese and Korean dialects:**
  - Dropped: kanji written over a dialect reading the source only romanizes (ja_kg, ja_kyo, ja_hak).
  - Dropped: ko_bus big (커다 is tagged 경북).
  - Kept: Korean dialect cells only where Robbeets gives the same word for that region; standard kana words (ja_kyo あげる, ja_hak やる) with a dialect source.
- **Whole rows dropped:**
  - egy: hieroglyphs converted from the Manuel de Codage by the drafter.
  - oar: general Aramaic forms, not attested for the stage.
  - jiu: tones disagree.
- **Other drops:**
  - English accent rows whose IPA did not follow the row (en_ck, en_est, en_aave small; en_scouse walk).
  - Noun-class mismatches: myx, nyo, yao, umb.
  - ps know (glossed 'understand').
  - Hunzib and Abkhaz IPA read from spelling.
  - syc big (the entry is a noun).
- **Kept: sourced homographs and colexifications.**
  - ro mare big/sea.
  - ce дала, evn буми give/die.
  - Clashes with child: lad chiko, nap piccerillo, mwl pequeinho, srn pikin, sux 𒌉 small/child.
  - wo mag big/elder.
  - haw ʻike know/see, haw ʻōlelo say/language.
  - tok toki.
  - Tibeto-Burman homophones: cng, hni, nmf, nzm, gqu, lic.
