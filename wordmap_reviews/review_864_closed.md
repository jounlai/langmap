## Trims (my decisions)
- **Routes dropped:**
  - say: Aramaic אמר (syc and myz had no etymology of their own); Sanskrit vadati (reached 4 only via the pedagogical sa_edu and pi_edu rows); Mongolic *kele- (reached 4 only via mn_cn as a script variant).

  - big: Proto-Philippine *dakél (Ilokano, Cebuano and Waray appear only in ACD's note); Proto-Semitic *rabb- (reached 4 only via aii and myz).
  - walk: Proto-Finnic *käüdäk (fi/olo/krl only via a derived verb); Proto-Bantu *-támb- (sw rests on one cognate set, ln is a loan); Sanskrit calati (pedagogical row).
- **Cells taken out:**

  - small: be маленькі ("cognate with" only).

  - know: kg, xh, zu, ssw out of *-jíjɪba (Grollemund files them in separate sets).
  - give: ksh jevve (matched by stage, not listed).
- **Relabelled:** know *qilo-a is Proto-Malayo-Polynesian (Pollex places it there), not Proto-Polynesian.
- **Left plain by the agents:**
