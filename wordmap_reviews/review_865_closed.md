## Process

  - Japanese/Korean dialect rows need a source for the region;
  - no kanji or Hangul written over a romanized-only reading;

  - two sources naming different words → skip.
- **Verification:** 12 verifiers, each told to re-read EVERY source (all reported 220–223 re-read).
