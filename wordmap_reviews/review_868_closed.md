# Review 868 — etymological routes for hot, cold, worm, nail, bite, burn

2026-11-28. Follows review 867. One route agent per word (grp/BRIEF_BASE.md, same brief as review 866). Every source was opened in the session: Wiktionary descendants lists, CDIAL, STEDT, ACD, Starling.

## Trims (my decisions)
- **Routes dropped:**
  - hot, Proto-Slavic *vьrǫťь, and bite, Proto-Slavic *gryzti: hr/sr/bs rest on one Serbo-Croatian entry; with sl that is two entries.
  - nail, Korean 손톱; nail, Proto-Japonic *tumai; worm, Proto-Vietic *p-luːn: each is one language with its own stages or dialects (the Middle Korean stage was not opened). The Japonic *atu of hot was already left plain on the same ground.
  - bite, Proto-Bantu *-dʊ́m-: reached 4 only by counting Kwanyama, which the source does not name.
  - bite, Arabic عَضَّ: Arabic varieties only.
- **Cells taken out:**
  - cold: ur, pnb out of *ṭhaṇḍha (CDIAL 13676 does not name them).
  - worm: ku kurm (the source lists kirm).
  - burn: ks out of PIE *dʰegʷʰ- (CDIAL lists it under the passive dahyátē); ar_sa (a Najdi row placed through the Hijazi section).
- **Kept:**
  - Persian garm loans in bn/ur/mr with Sorani (hot), each listed by Wiktionary.
  - Latin calēns and calidus as two routes (each has its own descendants list).
  - Latin cremāre with Vulgar Latin *caimāre (quemar/queimar), which the cremo page lists as derived.
  - Arabic بارد with Swahili baridi (cold) and حَرَقَ with Maltese (burn).
- **Left plain by the agents:** Bantu sets where BLR3 gives zones only; Sino-Tibetan *tsa 'hot' (only Tibetan tagged); Spanish gusano (origin uncertain); Malay gigit (ACD files it under two etyma); Turkic tooth-verbs for bite ("cf." only); Polynesian kati (hedged).
