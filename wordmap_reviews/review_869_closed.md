# Review 869 — today's 42 words against Wiktionary's translation tables

2026-11-28. Follows review 868. The owner asked to stop adding words and continue reviewing.

## Method
- For the 42 words added in reviews 853–867, every cell was compared with the English Wiktionary translation table of the matching sense: the first table, or "to travel through air" for fly and the two "consumed by fire" tables for burn.
- The comparison ignored simplified vs traditional characters, stress marks, Arabic/Hebrew/Syriac vowel points and apostrophe variants.
- 7,283 cells could be compared. 6,762 agree. 521 do not:
  - 220 are close spellings;
  - 241 are different words;
  - 60 have our form in another sense's table.
- 7 verifiers reopened the cell's own source for each of the 521, about 530 pages and dataset lookups in all. They also compared each cell with the row's habit in eat/drink/come and good/full.

## Findings
- **Almost all mismatches are not errors:**
  - the row's citation form: Latin and Ancient Greek infinitives, Somali imperative stems, Albanian 1sg, Tatar verbal nouns, Tagalog -um- verbs, Navajo 1sg;
  - the head-hair word, where the first table is for body hair or a single hair;
  - a variety's own form: Sahidic Coptic, Carpathian Rusyn, North Karelian;
  - script variants: Tifinagh, Sundanese;
  - a typo in the Wiktionary table: Ojibwe nishlanzh, Punjabi ਛੋੱਟਾ, Quechua cupa, Kikuyu mbogo "buffalo" given for neck.
- **Fixed:**
  - woman, Manggarai: inéwai → iné-wai, the spelling of the cited entry.
  - small, Vilamovian: klin → klīn /kliːn/, the entry's headword; the row marks long vowels.
- **Removed:** liver, Limburgish Léëver. It is the Eupen form (capitalised, /ʁ/) in a row of another variety.
- **Note corrected:** sit, Portuguese sentar-se. NorthEuraLex has estar sentado, so the note now cites Wiktionary sentar "(reflexive) to sit" instead.
- **Kept after a look:**
  - breast, Sicilian pettu, Neapolitan pietto, Venetian pèto: Wiktionary's first gloss is "chest", but the Romance Swadesh list (Saenko) files them under breast.
  - walk, Akkadian 𒀀𒆷𒆪𒌝: the attested spelling ends in -um, unlike the row's eat and drink.
  - horn, Yao livengwa: both cited sources list it first.

2 cells fixed, 1 removed, 1 note corrected.
