# Review 871 — transliteration left in the IPA field of proto-language rows

2026-11-28. Follows review 870, which held Proto-Semitic.

## Method
- Every row whose cells are reconstructions (*…) was scanned for transliteration letters in the IPA field (š ṣ ḥ ḳ ṭ ḏ ṯ ẓ ḫ ā ī ū ǰ ṇ y …).
- Each hit was compared with how the same row writes that sound in its other cells.

## Findings
- **Not defects:**
  - Proto-Turkic, Mongolic, Tungusic and Uralic *y*: it is the IPA vowel [y] for ü (kyn, tyn, kylmæ).
  - Proto-Austronesian S, C, N, R: the whole row uses Blust's symbols, as a uniform transliteration.
- **Fixed to the row's own convention (30 cells):**
  - **Proto-Semitic (20):** the row writes θ, ð, ħ, χ, ː, j and pharyngealised emphatics (sˤaɣiːr, θˤupr; ḳ as qʼ in naʃaqʼ).
    - water maːj, fire ʔiʃ, sun ʃamʃ, moon warχ, rain matˤar, tree ʕiɬˤ, leaf waraqʼ, bird ʕusˤːuːr, egg bajɬˤat, head raʔʃ;
    - eye ʕajn, I ʔanaːku, name ʃim, drink ʃataj, hear ʃamaʕ, good tˤaːb, one ʔaħad, two θin, meat ʃiʔr, house bajt.
    - ṣ́ is written ɬˤ, the lateral counterpart of the row's sˤ.
  - **Proto-Sino-Tibetan:** fire mej, sun nəj, dog d-kʷəj, like ɡ-ləj and ti(j).
  - **Proto-Bantu:** mother -ɲìná, drink -ɲóa, like -ɲéɲèdí.
  - **Proto-Dravidian:** drink kuʈi, two iraɳʈu, like naʈa and kaɳ.
  - **PIE:** see wejd, like wéj and tréjes.
  - **Proto-Afroasiatic:** eye ʕajn, like jamin.
  - **Proto-Kartvelian:** eat tʃʼam, like tsʼqʼal.
- After the fixes, no reconstruction row has a transliteration letter left in its IPA field, apart from the uniform Austronesian symbols.
