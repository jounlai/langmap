# Review 872 — the older 172 words against Wiktionary's translation tables, again

2026-11-28. Follows review 871. Review 835 (2026-11-25) made the first pass over these words.

## Method
- 143 words were compared with the English Wiktionary translation table of their sense. The table was chosen by hand where the first table is another sense: bone, kiss, fart, tear, orange, tomato, you and others.
- Greetings, interjections, country names and modern coinages were left out, because their tables are thin.
- 34,791 cells could be compared. 30,587 agree and 4,204 differ.
- Two groups were reopened at their sources:
  - **109** that differ and have changed since review 835;
  - **323** whose spelling is close to the table's but not the same (similarity ≥ 0.85, and not just a space, hyphen or apostrophe).
- 6 verifiers opened about 800 sources: Wiktionary, the lexibank datasets, dictionaries (Katersat, Plattmakers, Jacobson, du Plessis, focloir.ie, Ojibwe People's Dictionary), Bible texts and Wikipedias.

## Fixed (40 cells)
- **Misspellings against the source:**
  - Low German Kuukuuk "cuckoo" and Appelsien "orange";
  - Somali balanbaalis "butterfly";
  - Kashubian jaje "egg";
  - Piedmontese teramòt "earthquake" (teremòt is Emilian) and arcancel "rainbow";
  - Sena mwezi "moon";
  - Nuer ŋuaan "four" (ŋuan means "bad");
  - Kalenjin got "house";
  - Lingala likei "egg";
  - Maguindanao mapia "good";
  - Drekay Rukai balrilawlaw "rainbow" and acilay "water";
  - Arabana vapardla "head";
  - Kildin Sami та̄ххьт "bone" and пе̄тнэһць "Friday";
  - Lomwe etheneeri "star";
  - Tooro embaraasi "horse";
  - Tumbuka chilwa "island";
  - Wolof soraans "orange";
  - Kiribati koobe "coffee";
  - Igbo sinoo "snow";
  - Jamaican Patois chaaklit "chocolate";
  - Tofa өғ "house";
  - Greenlandic tuusinti "thousand".
- **Tone or script:**
  - Iu Mien nziaamv "blood": the final h meant the wrong tone; Chen 2012 gives tone 3.
  - Dzongkha རྒྱལ་པོ "king": the tsheg between syllables restored.
  - Middle Mongol ᠵᠢᠭᠠᠰᠤᠨ "fish": the script had dropped the final n that its IPA has.
  - Caucasian Albanian bˁeɣ "sun": the pharyngeal restored.
  - Chadian Arabic اسم "name" and المي "water", as in the SIL lexicon.
  - Sorani ھ for /h/ in honey, egg and week, as in the row's other 9 cells.
- **Other word or form:**
  - Acehnese buleuën "moon": the standard form.
  - Chamorro trongkon håyu "tree": ACD after Topping. Bare trongkon is the bound form of "trunk".
  - Saterland Frisian träi "three": the masculine lemma, like twäin "two".
  - Ladino indiana "turkey": only the feminine is attested.
  - Zhuang 姆 "mother": listed in Wiktionary's Sawndip glyphs for meh, while 𫱆 is not. 𭑫, listed first, would need the Sawndip font subset rebuilt from the full font.
  - Zhuang "sun": the romanised alt daengngoenz; daengzngoenz contradicts its own tone.

## Removed (6 cells)
- Old Prussian katto "cat" (only Paustocatto "wild cat" is attested; the cell is now "—") and tūsimtons "thousand" (an accusative plural, possibly an ordinal).
- Tsonga "butterfly": Cuenod, Wiktionary and the JW text give three spellings, and the cell's own dictionary could not be opened.
- Kiribati tiiti "cheese", Blackfoot "sugar" and Comorian "honey": no source can be opened, and the cell does not match the table.

## Not applied
- **Zhuang 火 "fire", 日 "sun", 月 "moon":** the verifier proposed other glyphs or Latin. The owner approved these meaning-based Sawndip glyphs (commit 770381484), and a lone Latin cell would break the native-script row rule.
- **Kept on weaker evidence:** the Luhya eshi- forms (the Wanga Bible site refused automated access), Algonquin eyèbig and Hiri Motu ororo (print-only sources).
