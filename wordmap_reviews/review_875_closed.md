# Review 875 — the Aleut row rebuilt in sourced Alaskan Latin

2026-11-28. Follows review 874, which held Aleut.

## Finding
- The row had 66 cells in Cyrillic with no per-cell sources and 18 in Alaskan Latin.
- The Cyrillic spelling (х̆ к̆ г̆, doubled vowels, plain д for ð) matches no published orthography:
  - Golovko's Bering-Island spelling writes ӽ ӄ ӷ and macrons;
  - the 19th-century spelling writes х̑ г̑.
- The Cyrillic cells reproduce NorthEuraLex's Latin form for form, so they were transliterations.

## Rebuild
- Every cell now comes from a source, in the Alaskan standard orthography (Bergsland 1994), Eastern dialect as the row's description says. NorthEuraLex is Eastern: aalax "two", qaankun "three", tingin "we".
- **Sources:**
  - **NorthEuraLex:** 77 cells. The hyphens that mark morpheme breaks are dropped, x́ is written x̂ and ǵ is written ĝ.
  - **Wiktionary (Bergsland), the UCLA Eastern Aleut list (1996) and ASJP:** cited where they agree. cat kuuskax̂, whale alax̂, soap miilax̂, hello aang and thanks qaĝaalakux̂ (Eastern) come from these.
- **IPA:** phonemic, as the row's existing Latin cells and Wiktionary's Aleut entries write it: x̂ χ, ĝ ʁ, g ɣ, d ð, doubled vowel ː.
- **Old cells that held another word:**
  - king tukux̂ "chief, rich" → kurulax̂;
  - we тимис → tingin;
  - eat → inulix, good → iĝamanalix, love → qumyuxtalix;
  - tree → hyaagax̂;
  - cat кошкаах̆ (Russian koshka) → kuuskax̂;
  - earth tanax̂ "land" → chiqix̂ "soil", which is the map's sense.
- **Small fixes to cells already in Latin:** ǵ → ĝ in blue, green, left and right; tea chaayux̂; ear tutusix̂.
- The script filter now lists the row under Latin only (scriptTags:['Latin']). meta.script keeps the language's history, Cyrillic on the Commander Islands included.
- The row's "bound stem" flags for drink and love are removed, since both are now full -lix forms.
- **name:** NorthEuraLex asax-x́, written asaxx̂ by the same mapping. No source writes it otherwise, and the map needs a cell for every living language.
- **tear = water (taangax̂):** NorthEuraLex files this one word under both meanings. Locked as a sourced colexification.

## Kept although sources differ
- stone nux̂ (NorthEuraLex, ASJP) against nuĝix̂ (UCLA, Wiktionary).
- snow qaniix (NorthEuraLex) against qaniigix (Wiktionary).
- rain kimdux against chix̂tax̂: the latter is probably Atkan.
- Each time, the Eastern source was preferred.
